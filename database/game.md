# Path of Exile 2 — Game Overview

> Snapshot: 2026-11-17 · Current patch: 0.5.x · Full release 1.0: **December 11, 2026**

**Path of Exile 2** is Grinding Gear Games' action RPG sequel, set ~20 years after the events of *Path of Exile 1*. It shares the endgame-agnostic account economy model with PoE 1 (cosmetics and points carry over), but is a separate game with its own campaign, skill system, and passive tree.

## Core structure

- **Campaign:** Acts 1–4 + 3 Interludes in 0.5.x; Acts 5–6 (Oriath) complete the six-act story at 1.0.
- **Endgame:** Atlas of Worlds with Waystone maps, Atlas Passive Tree (condensed to ~70 nodes at 1.0), pinnacle bosses ([bosses/index.md](bosses/index.md)).
- **Classes:** 8 playable in 0.5.x → 10 at 1.0 (Duelist, Shadow) → 12 total post-1.0 (Marauder, Templar pending). See [classes/](classes/).
- **Ascendancies:** 22 in 0.5.x (+ Abyssal Lich variant) → 26 at 1.0. See [ascendancies/](ascendancies/).
- **Systems:** Attributes (Str/Dex/Int), Spirit reservation resource, skill gems with directly socketed supports — see [mechanics/index.md](mechanics/index.md).

## Monetization

Free-to-play from 1.0 onward. Income from optional cosmetics and stash tabs (PoE 1 model). EA supporter packs retired at 1.0; all purchases carry over permanently. 1.0 core supporter packs: Archon of Rebirth $60, Ash $100, Destruction $160, Divinity $240, Corruption $480 (upgradable).

## Key facts

- 8+ years of development (started ~2018, announced ExileCon 2019)
- 320+ skills, 400+ uniques, 150+ maps at 1.0 (press figures)
- PoE 1 continues side-by-side (Covens of Umbra 3.30 launched Nov 13, 2026)
- Post-1.0 cadence: ~4-month league cycles toward 12 classes / 36 ascendancies
