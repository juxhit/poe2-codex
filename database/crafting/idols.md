# Idols — Acquisition & Rerolling

> Full mechanics: [mechanics/idols-jewels-charms-slots.md](../mechanics/idols-jewels-charms-slots.md) · Catalog: [items/idols.md](../items/idols.md)

- Dropped by **Azmerian Spirit-possessed monsters** (Hunting Grounds onward; "Those Who Hear" passive: +50% idol drop chance in Lv75+ areas).
- 8 new idols added in 0.5.0; all socketable in **Sceptres** as well as the Idols panel.
- **No reroll currency exists** `(unconfirmed — community consensus: idols cannot be rerolled; improved only by recombining duplicates)`.
- Replacing a socketed idol **destroys the original**; most idols are Limited: 1 per character.
- Ritual pinnacle **Queen in the Mists** drops three corrupted Idols.
- Glorious/Grand/Golden Idols serve as quest turn-ins.
