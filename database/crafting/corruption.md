# Corruption (Vaal Orbs)

> Sources: [Maxroll corruption outcomes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/corruption-outcomes), [poe2db — Corrupted](https://poe2db.tw/us/Corrupted), [Mobalytics vaal corrupting](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/vaal-corrupting)

Corruption is **permanent**: a corrupted item/gem can't take further currency (can still socket Augments; corrupted gems can still level).

## Equipment (Magic/Rare) outcomes

- Nothing happens
- 1–3 affixes randomized
- Add a Corrupted enchantment
- (Helmets/gloves/boots/chests/shields/focuses/martial weapons) **+1 Socket ignoring the limit**

## Uniques

- Affixes divined within ~0.78–1.22× of current roll, or enchantment added.
- Unique Jewels: 50% no change.

## Skill gems

- Nothing; +1/−1 level; +1/−1 support socket; −7% to +3% quality.
- Vaal skill conversions mentioned by community sources `(unconfirmed)`.

## Related corruption systems

- **Omen of Corruption** eliminates the "nothing happens" outcome (omen removed from drop pool in 0.5.0).
- **Altars of Corruption** (Act 3): corrupt one item for free.
- **Vaal Infuser** (Atziri's Temple): adds 1–2% quality up to 30% or non-randomizing corruption (needs ≥20% quality for other uses).
- **Architect's Orb**: 50/50 second enchantment or item destruction.
- **Vaal Cultivation Orb** (Fate of the Vaal): replaces up to 2 mods on Vaal Uniques.
- **Crystallised Corruption** (gems): second corruption outcome, 50/50 to destroy the gem.
- Corrupted-equip payoffs exist (e.g. "With 5 Corrupted Items Equipped: Gain Soul Eater for 10s on Vaal Skill use" — poe2db).
