# 1.0 Crafting — Conquerors of Wraeclast (Dec 11, 2026)

> Sources: [PoE Vault league explainer](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/news/conquerors-of-wraeclast-explained-path-of-exile-2s-1-0-league-has-you-raise-an-army), [Maxroll ExileCon 2026 recap](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/exilecon-2026-recap), [Mobalytics 1.0 summary](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/1-0-livestream-exilecon-content-summary)

- Launches **December 11, 2026** with Acts 5–6, Shadow and Duelist classes.
- League: recruit troops/commanders (Varek), build strongholds (Karui Island / Ezomyte River Keep / Maraketh Desert Rampart, 20+ buildings).
- **Treasury** (boss-key loot room) is the **only source** of the league crafting materials.

## New materials

| Material | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Crimson Iron** | Selects a modifier to become **Forgemarked**; reforging the item replaces it with a random new one that becomes **permanently Fractured** |
| **Infused Blood** | Deterministically adds brand-new exclusive modifiers (e.g. Chaos skills apply "Engulfed in Darkness" on hit; Stealth strikes apply "Corrupted Blood" — shown for Shadow) |

- Materials also let players reroll unwanted modifiers and "discover brand new mods" (Mobalytics).
- Additional crafting specifics not fully revealed pre-1.0 `(unconfirmed)` — refresh this file after Dec 11 patch notes.
