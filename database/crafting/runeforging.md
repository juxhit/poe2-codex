# Runeforging (0.5.0 "Runes of Aldur")

> Sources: [Maxroll 0.5.0 notes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-0-patch-notes-return-of-the-ancients), [Mobalytics Runes of Aldur](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/runes-of-aldur), [Fextralife Runeforging Guide](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Runeforging_Guide)

## Remnants (league mechanic)

- Each area contains an **Ezomyte Remnant** — choose a combo of **Runes/Currency**, then fight Rune-empowered waves.
- Bigger combos (more slots/runeshapes, 2–10 slots) = rarer rewards (Tempest+Lightning → Orb of Augmentation; five-Rune combos → socketable Runes).
- **Runic Recipes** placed in Remnant sockets are the league's deterministic crafting.

## Verisium & the Anvil

- **Verisium** drops from Remnant monsters; fuels **Verisium Runeforging** at the **Verisium Anvil**.
- Unlocked in Act 1 via NPC **Farrow** ("The Runeseeker" quest — Lost Catacombs behind the inscribed door in Grim Tangle).
- Applies **Runic Ward** (defence pool: triggers at 1 Life, regenerates independently) to armour; armours under ilvl 55 get it free, above 55 trade base defences for Ward.
- Act 3 unlocks runeforged **Unique armour base upgrades**; Act 2 Titan Runic Vault; recipes for Unique Jewellery/Armour/Weapons found in Grand Expeditions' underground areas.
- Powers **40+ Kalguuran Runic Skills/Buffs/Supports** (no attribute requirements; 21 skills + 8 supports).
- Hub: The Ruins of Kingsmarch (Logbooks replaced by ocean exploration / Grand Expeditions).

## Runic Alloys

- **13 alloy currency items** (Act 2 quest): each *removes a random modifier and augments a Rare with a guaranteed mod obtainable only via alloys* (like Perfect Essences).
- Types (poe2db): Runic, Adaptive, Protective, Expansive, Swift, Cyclonic, Prismatic, Mystic, Sovereign, Celestial, Transcendent, The Runebinder's, The Runefather's Alloys.
- Examples: Runic Alloy (Ring +37–49 max Runic Ward); Celestial Alloy (+1 to all Spell Skills).

## Fluxes

- **3 Flux currencies**: transform an item's resistances from one element to another.
