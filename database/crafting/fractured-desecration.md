# Fractured Items & Desecration

> Sources: [Mobalytics fracturing orbs](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/fracturing-orbs), [poe2db — Desecrated Modifiers](https://poe2db.tw/us/Desecrated_Modifiers), [0.5.0 notes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-0-patch-notes-return-of-the-ancients), [PoE Vault 1.0 league](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/news/conquerors-of-wraeclast-explained-path-of-exile-2s-1-0-league-has-you-raise-an-army)

!!! warning "Fracturing locks a *random* mod — permanently"
    The Fracturing Orb locks one random mod of the 4+ on the Rare, and fractured mods can never be changed. Chaos-spam into the desired layout *first*, then fracture (0.5.0: requires an Atlas specialisation node).

## Fracturing Orb

- Usable on a Rare with **4+ explicit modifiers**; permanently fractures ("locks") one **random** mod.
- Fractured mods can never be changed.
- Common strategy: Chaos-spam until the desired mod layout, then fracture.
- 0.5.0: requires an Atlas specialisation node to use.

## Abyss Desecration (exclusive mods, not fractures)

- **Abyssal Bones** desecrate an item, adding an *Unrevealed Desecrated modifier* (removing a random mod if full).
- Revealed via the **Well of Souls** / Gnawed Bones; Desecrated mods are exclusive affixes controlled by the Abyss omens — see [omens.md](omens.md).

## 1.0 "Forgemarked" reforging (Conquerors of Wraeclast)

- Treasury-only materials — **Crimson Iron** selects a modifier to become *Forgemarked*.
- Reforging the item then replaces that modifier with a random new one that becomes **permanently Fractured**.
- **Infused Blood** vials add specific new modifiers instead of random ones (e.g. Chaos skills apply "Engulfed in Darkness" on hit; Stealth strikes apply "Corrupted Blood" — shown for Shadow).
- Details: [1-0-crafting.md](1-0-crafting.md)
