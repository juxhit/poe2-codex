# Benches, Recipes & Trade

> Sources: [poewiki vendor recipe system](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Vendor_recipe_system) (PoE1 ref), [Reddit 3-to-1 list](https://www.reddit.com/r/PathOfExile2/comments/1hjtjfg/), [Game8 reforging bench](https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/490017), [Mobalytics currency exchange](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/currency-exchange), [GGG async trade FAQ](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3828185)

!!! tip "Waystone upgrading & free-sell trading"
    Reforge **3 same-tier waystones → 1 higher tier** for Atlas sustain; on trade, hideout **Merchant Tabs** charge the gold fee to the *buyer* — listing is free for the seller.

## Salvage Bench

- Gear → **Artificer's Shards** / quality currency; socketed gear → Artificer's Shards; Uniques have special salvage outcomes.

## Reforging Bench

- **3 items of the same base type → 1 new item** with rerolled mods (output ilvl = lowest input).
- Also 3→1 for runes (tier upgrades), catalysts, flasks/charms.
- 3 same-tier **waystones → 1 higher tier** — see [mechanics/atlas-waystones.md](../mechanics/atlas-waystones.md).
- Post-1.0: fractured/Forgemarked mods interact with reforging ([fractured-desecration.md](fractured-desecration.md)).

## Vendor recipes

- PoE2 has **no classic PoE1 vendor recipe set** — no confirmed Chaos Orb "full rare set" recipe `(unconfirmed whether any hidden full-set payouts exist)`.
- Recipe-like functionality moved to the Salvage/Reforging benches above.

## Trade systems

- **Faustus (Currency Exchange):** instant market-rate bulk currency swaps; buy/sell orders cost **Gold** (scales with value).
- NPCs: Finn (Act 1 Clearfell), Risu (Act 2), Alva (Act 3 + hideout); Ange (0.5.5-era exchange/market NPC).
- **Ange / Merchant Tabs (0.3.0):** asynchronous item trading — list gear in hideout Merchant Tabs at your price; **buyers pay the gold fee, sellers pay nothing**.
- Item-for-item: [pathofexile.com/trade2](https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2) + whisper/one-click pickup.
