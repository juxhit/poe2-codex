# Quality & Sockets

> Sources: [Mobalytics currency guide](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/currency-guide), [Fextralife Artificer's Orb](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Artificer%27s_Orb), [Maxroll runes & soul cores](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-and-soul-cores)

## Quality currency (max 20%)

| Currency | Applies to | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith's Whetstone | Martial weapons | 1% more physical damage per quality |
| Arcanist's Etcher | Wands/staves/sceptres | Spirit on sceptres |
| Armourer's Scrap | Armour | 1% more defences |
| Gemcutter's Prism | Gems | +5% quality |
| Glassblower's Bauble | Flasks | Quality → capacity |

- Obtained via drops and the **Salvage Bench** (salvage "Superior" items).
- Vaal Infuser can push an item's quality up to 30%.
- Vaal Infusers / Temple mechanics require **≥20% quality** items.

## Augment sockets (Artificer's Orb)

- **Artificer's Orb** adds an Augment socket (10 Artificer's Shards from salvaging socketed gear = 1 orb).
- Max sockets: Body Armour 2, Two-handed 2, One-handed 1, Helmet/Gloves/Boots 1.
- Rare high-tier drops can exceed by 1; **Vaal Orb** can add a socket beyond the cap; Morior Invictus has extra sockets.

## Skill gem support sockets

- No PoE1-style links — supports socket directly into skills via **Jeweller's Orbs** (Lesser/Greater/Perfect → 2/3/4 sockets) — see [currency-crafting.md](currency-crafting.md).
