# Jewel Crafting

> Sources: [0.5.0 patch notes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-0-patch-notes-return-of-the-ancients), [Mobalytics distilled emotions](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/distilled-emotions), [Timesaver Time-Lost guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-time-lost-jewels-guide)

## Basic jewels

- Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Cobalt, etc. — socket into the passive tree; Magic/Rare crafted with the standard currency loop ([currency-crafting.md](currency-crafting.md)).
- 0.5.0 tweaks: Ruby can roll +1–2 Maximum Rage; Sapphire 7–13% increased Chaos Damage; "Fevered" prefix removed from Time-Lost Sapphire.

## Catalysts

- From the **Genesis Tree** (Breach): add quality to Jewels.
- 12 new catalysts in 0.5.0; interacts with Catalysing Exaltation omen ([omens.md](omens.md)).

## Emotions (Delirium currency)

- **Liquid Emotions**: instill notables on amulets; in 0.5.0 also **craft mods on Jewels like Greater Essences**.
- 3 new **Potent Emotions** craft exclusive jewel mods and instill 16 new notables.
- Loathsome Mire amulets: two instilled notables at −1 prefix or −1 suffix.
- **10 Ancient Liquid Emotions** craft **Time-Lost Jewels** (plus 3 Ancient Potent emotions); other **Ancient Emotions** remove/add mods on Time-Lost Jewels only.

## Time-Lost Jewels

- Large-radius jewels with powerful passives.
- Sources: **Time-Lost Cache** at the end of the Trial of the Sekhemas, world drops, Ancient Emotions crafting (0.5.0).
- Disciple of Varashta: Baryanic Leylines +40% radius on non-unique Time-Lost Jewels.
