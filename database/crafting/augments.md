# Augment Crafting (Runes & Soul Cores)

> Socketing mechanics/rules: [mechanics/augments-sockets.md](../mechanics/augments-sockets.md) · Catalog with values: [items/runes-soulcores.md](../items/runes-soulcores.md)

## Crafting-relevant rules

- Runes: **3 identical → 1 of next tier** at the Reforging Bench (Lesser → Normal → Greater). Endgame Runes cannot be upgraded.
- Some 0.5.0 runes **alter crafting options** (e.g. Uhtred's Sidereus Rune enables Chronomancy modifiers that otherwise can't roll).
- 0.5.0 adds **60+ runes made by destroying a Unique**.
- Soul Cores cannot be crafted — dropped from Trial of Chaos (~52 cores in 0.5.5); **Core Destabiliser** (Atziri's Temple) converts one into a stronger Ancient Soul Core (may destroy it).
- Abyssal Eyes (Ulaman's/Amanamu's Gaze) drop from Abyss content; 1 Ancient Augment per character.
- Socketed Augment replacement **destroys the old one**; **Orb of Extraction** saves the Augment but destroys the item.
