# Essences

> Sources: [poe2db — Essence](https://poe2db.tw/us/Essence), [Fextralife Essences](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Essences), [Timesaver essence guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-essence-guide)

## Tiers & rules

- Four tiers: **Lesser, Normal, Greater, Perfect**.
- **Lesser / Normal / Greater**: upgrade a **Magic** item to Rare, adding one **guaranteed modifier** (tier- and slot-scaled — a targeted Regal).
- **Perfect** (and **Corrupt**) essences: used on **Rare** items — *remove a random modifier and add a new guaranteed one*.
- Essence monsters (frozen, buffed enemies) drop the displayed essence; Vaal Orbs can corrupt imprisoned essence monsters → upgrade to Greater or convert to Corrupted Essences.
- 0.5.0: campaign essence count increased; Essence of the Infinite drop share decreased. "Essence of the Workshop" referenced by community sites but not in poe2db `(unconfirmed)`.

## Core essences (guarantees per slot)

| Essence | Guarantee |
|---|---|
| the Body | +Life; Perfect: Body Armour 8–10% increased max Life |
| the Mind | +Mana; Perfect: Ring 4–6% increased max Mana |
| Enhancement | +% Armour/Evasion/ES; Perfect: Amulet 20–30% increased Global defences |
| Abrasion | Flat Physical; Perfect: 15–20% (1H/Bow) / 25–33% (2H/Xbow) of Damage as Extra Physical |
| Flames / Ice / Electricity | Flat Fire/Cold/Lightning (same structure as Abrasion) |
| Ruin | Chaos Resistance; Perfect: Body Armour 10–15% of Physical Damage taken as Chaos |
| Battle | Accuracy; Perfect: +2 (1H/Bow) / +3 (2H/Xbow) to Level of all Attack Skills |
| Sorcery | Spell Damage; Perfect: Wand +3 / Staff +5 to Level of all Spell Skills |
| Haste | Attack Speed; Perfect: 20–25% chance to gain Onslaught on Killing Hits |
| the Infinite | +Str/Dex/Int; Perfect: Amulet 7–10% increased Str/Dex/Int |
| Seeking | Crit chance / spell crit; Perfect: Body Armour — Hits against you have 40–50% reduced Critical Damage Bonus |
| Insulation / Thawing / Grounding | Fire/Cold/Lightning Res; Perfect: 26–30% of that element's damage taken Recouped as Life (Belt/Helmet/Gloves) |
| Alacrity | Cast Speed; Perfect: 18–20% (Focus/Wand) / 28–32% (Staff) increased Mana Cost Efficiency |
| Opulence | Increased Rarity; Perfect: Gloves 10–15% increased Gold dropped |
| Command | Allies in Presence deal increased Damage (Sceptre line); Perfect: Aura Skills 15–20% increased Magnitudes |

## Corrupt / special essences

- **Hysteria** — slot-specific strong mods (Boots 30% Movement Speed, Gloves 25–29% Crit Damage Bonus)
- **Delirium** — Body Armour allocates a random Notable
- **Horror** — Gloves/Boots: 60% increased effect of Socketed Augment Items
- **Insanity** — Belt: on Corruption gains two Enchantments
- **the Abyss** — Mark of the Abyssal Lord
- **the Breach** — Jewellery +20% Maximum Quality
