# Druid

- **Attributes:** Strength/Intelligence (hybrid)
- **Starting stats:** Str 11 / Dex 7 / Int 11 (unconfirmed — follows hybrid pattern)
- **Weapons:** Animal Talismans (select shapeshift form) + staff/two-handed options in human form
- **Sources:** [Fextralife — Druid](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Druid) · [Official 0.4.0 notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3883495) · [pathofexile2.com/druids](https://pathofexile2.com/druids)

## Playstyle

A shapeshifting elemental spellcaster who fluidly switches between human spellcasting and beast forms — casting a bear skill transforms him; casting a spell reverts to human (with a minor transformation penalty). Forms synergize with spells, e.g., human summons a volcano and bear slams to reactivate it. Added in patch 0.4.0 "The Last of the Druids" (December 2025).

## Class mechanic

**Shapeshifting** between Bear / Wolf / Wyvern and human form (forms chosen per Animal Talisman); Bear form builds **Rage** from attacks; transformation is fueled by "Draiocht" (per Fextralife — unconfirmed as the official resource name). The **Talisman** mechanic lets any class shapeshift.

## Skill kit

Primal Skills (~20+ in 0.4.0): Bear (slams, roars, Rage), Wolf (bleed/lunge), Wyvern (lightning explosions, fire spit, flight); human-form spells (volcano/lava geysers, lightning storms, vines).
