# Future Classes

PoE 2 targets **12 classes / 36 ascendancies**. Status as of 2026-11-17: 8 playable (0.5.x), **Duelist + Shadow ship with 1.0 (Dec 11, 2026)**; Marauder and Templar follow post-1.0.

## Duelist — 1.0 (CONFIRMED)

- **Attributes:** Str/Dex hybrid; starting Str 11 / Dex 11 / Int 7; starts bottom-middle of the passive tree (same position as Mercenary)
- **Weapons:** Swords — one-handed (dual-wield, or + shield/sceptre) or two-handed
- **Mechanic — Stance system:** **Ready Stance** (entered by channeling Bloodied Blade) empowers skills into **Blood Stance** (more damage, e.g., Blood Nova) or **Sand Stance** (larger AoE, extra Sand Disks). Key skills: Bloodied Blade, Perforate, Blade Conduit, Cyclone. Press coverage reports Sand Stance triggers on stuns and Blood Stance on parries/bleeds (not in GGG's own text).
- **Ascendancies:**
  - **The Knight** — recruits a **Squire** companion who equips gear and any melee Skill Gem; confirmed day-one
  - **Honourbound** — Glory generates Honour, strikes send justice waves
  - **Bannerlord** — War Banners themed on old empires, incl. a Vaal Warbanner
  - **Glorious Entrance** — mounts the Liberator's ghostly chariot
  - **Master-at-Arms** — teaser only ("an expert in a thousand forms of combat"); source conflict on day-one status (GGG Steam post says both launch in 1.0; GGG press text says "later date")
- **Sources:** [Mobalytics — Duelist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/duelist) · [Maxroll 1.0 news](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/everything-new-in-path-of-exile-2-1-0) · [Official deep dive](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3999858)

## Shadow — 1.0 (CONFIRMED)

- **Attributes:** Dex/Int hybrid
- **Weapons:** Daggers + traps
- **Mechanics:** **Stealth** (dash-to-smoke; monsters can't see you until close range; cooldown resets on kill; Stealth empowers dagger skills); **8 trap skills** detonating in the order thrown; summons **Shades** that copy your attacks; a **Darkness** resource and **Eclipse** (temporary shadow realm). Returning skills: Ethereal Knives, Blade Vortex.
- **Ascendancies:** **Assassin** and **Infiltrator** (Mobalytics + AccountShark; Maxroll reported "Saboteur" — source conflict, 2 of 3 say Infiltrator)
- **Sources:** [Maxroll 1.0 news](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/everything-new-in-path-of-exile-2-1-0) · [Mobalytics — Shadow](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/shadow)

## Marauder — post-1.0 (unrevealed)

- **Attributes:** Strength (presumed pure)
- **Fantasy:** Front-line Karui axe-wielder; community expects pure Strength melee; one recurring guess is the class may not even keep the "Marauder" name (speculation — see [1.0/rumors.md](../1.0/rumors.md))
- **Ascendancies:** unknown

## Templar — post-1.0 (unrevealed)

- **Attributes:** Str/Int (presumed)
- **Fantasy:** Flails, holy magic, summoning; "more hybrid than melee" (community guesses)
- **Ascendancies:** unknown
