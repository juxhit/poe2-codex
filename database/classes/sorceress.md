# Sorceress

- **Attributes:** Intelligence (pure)
- **Starting stats:** Str 7 / Dex 7 / Int 15
- **Weapons:** No signature weapon (staves/wands/foci common)
- **Sources:** [Fextralife — Sorceress](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Sorceress) · [Maxroll class overview](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/poe-2-class-overview)

## Playstyle

The quintessential elemental mage and glass cannon — weaves blended Fire, Cold and Lightning spells from a distance, freezing, shocking and igniting packs. Designed for blending elements and trigger-gem automation plus weapon-swap dual specialization. Heavily mana-dependent with little personal defence.

## Class mechanic

No unique named resource. Primary user of **Trigger gems** (Cast on Ignite/Shock) and elemental ailment interactions; some basic attack skills (e.g., Fireball on wands) are weapon-tied and cast without mana.

## Skill kit

Fire/Ignite (Fireball, Flame Wall), Cold/Freeze (Comet, Ice Nova, Frost Bomb), Lightning/Shock (Spark, Shockchain). ~25 elemental skills at EA launch.
