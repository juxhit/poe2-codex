# Witch

- **Attributes:** Intelligence (pure)
- **Starting stats:** Str 7 / Dex 7 / Int 15
- **Weapons:** One-handed weapons (wands) + focus/off-hand
- **Sources:** [Fextralife — Witch](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Witch) · [Maxroll class overview](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/poe-2-class-overview)

## Playstyle

PoE2's necromancer — commands an army of persistent undead that auto-resummon after death while casting chaos and bone magic from safety. Corpses on the battlefield fuel temporary minions and plague effects, and many minions have their own active command skills. Safe, powerful, beginner-friendly; fragile body, can feel passive.

## Class mechanic

**Spirit** resource — the pool that pays for both persistent buffs and permanent minions; improved minion control (Command skills) and the Spectre system that "captures" slain enemies in skill gems.

## Skill kit

Minions (Skeletal Warriors, Skeletal Arsonists, Skeletal Snipers, Spectres), Chaos spells (Contagion), Curses, Osteomancy (Bone Offering, bone spikes). New for 1.0: **Soul Extraction**.
