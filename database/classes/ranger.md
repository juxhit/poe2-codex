# Ranger

- **Attributes:** Dexterity (pure)
- **Starting stats:** Str 7 / Dex 15 / Int 7
- **Weapons:** Bows (+ off-hand Quiver)
- **Sources:** [Fextralife — Ranger](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Ranger) · [Maxroll class overview](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/poe-2-class-overview)

## Playstyle

Quick, deadly archer who peppers enemies with arrows from range and vaults away before they close in. Shoots while moving and layers slows, freezes and poisons for crowd control. Fragile up close — positioning and mobility skills are the survival kit.

## Class mechanic

No unique named resource; signature tech is the **quiver off-hand slot** (extra offensive/defensive mods) and bow skills with integrated repositioning (shoot while moving, backward-leap shots).

## Skill kit

Lightning (Lightning Rod, Lightning Arrow), Ice (Ice Shot, freezing arrows), Poison (Vine Arrow, Poisonous Bloom), Physical shots; movement skills with built-in jumps/vaults.
