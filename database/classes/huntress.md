# Huntress

- **Attributes:** Dexterity (official — pure DEX per GGG's Dawn of the Hunt page; spear gear/skills carry Strength requirements, so some sources describe her as a Dex/Str hybrid — community contention, not official pairing)
- **Starting stats:** Str 7 / Dex 15 / Int 7
- **Weapons:** Spears (melee + thrown/returning) + Bucklers with parry
- **Sources:** [pathofexile2.com/hunt](https://pathofexile2.com/hunt) (official) · [Fextralife — Huntress](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Huntress) · [Mobalytics — Huntress overview](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/huntress-overview)

## Playstyle

An exiled Azmeri spear-wielder who darts in and out of battle, blending melee strikes with ranged spear throws and detonations. Combo-heavy and position-focused: set up wind/elemental zones, then trigger them on the way out. High-execution class — parry timing is central to survivability; comparatively squishy. Added in 0.2.0 "Dawn of the Hunt" (April 2025).

## Class mechanic

**Buckler parry** (timing-based counterplay) and spear **combo/detonation skills**. Rhoa mount combat appeared in the Dawn of the Hunt showcase (unconfirmed in live game).

## Skill kit

Whirling Slash, Twister combo, Wall of Spears, electrified spear detonations, bleed synergy, Solaris-themed holy damage options.
