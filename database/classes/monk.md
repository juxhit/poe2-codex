# Monk

- **Attributes:** Dexterity/Intelligence (hybrid)
- **Starting stats:** Str 7 / Dex 11 / Int 11
- **Weapons:** Quarterstaves (signature) + Unarmed finishers
- **Sources:** [Fextralife — Monk](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Monk) · [Maxroll class overview](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/poe-2-class-overview)

## Playstyle

Fast and furious martial artist who dashes in and out of combat, building and spending momentum. Swift staff strikes set up unarmed palm-finishers that trigger elemental explosions. High execution ceiling: fluid and flashy, but fragile in melee and dependent on keeping charges up.

## Class mechanic

**Power Charges** (generated and spent to enhance skills), momentum/combo build-up, and the summonable **Tempest Bell** that rings for extra damage when struck.

## Skill kit

Lightning/Ice/Wind martial arts (Tempest Flurry, Icicle Strike, Wave of Frost, Wind Blast), Killing Palm, Herald-style effects, Tempest Bell.
