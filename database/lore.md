# Lore — Brief Overview

> Spoiler-light summary. PoE2 is set ~20 years after *Path of Exile 1* (the death of the Beast of Wraeclast). Interpretations marked `(unconfirmed)` where fan-derived.

## World state

- The god-Beast of Wraeclast was slain at the end of PoE1 — but divinity did not die clean: the "twelve gone, thirteen remain" prophecy frames the game's god-killing theme (Count Geonor and other act bosses channel resurrected/divine power).
- Wraeclast's colonial powers and old empires shape the campaign: **Ogham** (Act 1, Ezomyte/Kalguur-adjacent frontier), the **Vastiri desert** (Act 2, Maraketh ruins), the buried **Vaal empire of Utzaal** (Act 3), the Kalguuran colony **Kingsmarch** (Act 4).
- **The Interludes** bridge to the endgame; **Acts 5–6 (1.0)** move the war to **Oriath** — the Templar theocracy capital from PoE1 — against the **Twilight Order** and the resurrected **Beast**.

## Factions & peoples

| Faction | Role |
|---|---|
| **Vaal** | Fallen empire of blood sacrifice; their ruins, soul cores and ultimatums permeate the endgame |
| **Kalguur** | Dwarf-like smith-clan; runeforging, Kingsmarch, Verisium |
| **Azmeri** | Wildwood spirit-tenders; Wisps, idols, the Huntress's people |
| **Ezomyte / Maraketh / Karui** | PoE1-legacy peoples; faction endgame picks at 1.0 (castle sieges) |
| **Templar / Twilight Order** | Oriath's orthodoxy — the Witchhunter and the 1.0 campaign antagonists |
| **Brethren of the Sun / Moon, Solaris & Lunaris** | God pair tied to Huntress spear arts and Act 2 iconography |
| **The First Ones / ancestral spirits** | Karui ancestor worship — Titan/Warbringer flavor `(unconfirmed naming)` |

## Player character

- You are an **Exile** — a castaway condemned to Wraeclast; the campaign builds an army against the coming Oriath invasion (Act 5 opens the gate).
- Endgame (0.5.x): the **Fortress**, the **Blood Lord and the Sanguar bloodline**, castle sieges across the Atlas (1.0).
