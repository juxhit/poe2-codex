# Waystones, Tablets & Keys

> Sources: [poe2db — Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones), [poe2db 0.5.0](https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.0). Loop mechanics: [mechanics/atlas-waystones.md](../mechanics/atlas-waystones.md)

## Waystones

- **16 tiers**: Tier 1 = area level 65 → Tier 16 = area level 80.
- Map bosses drop +1 tier; last rare in a map can drop same tier.
- Rerollable with Chaos Orbs; **Omens** constrain reroll outcomes; **Preserved Vertebrae** desecrates (Abyss mods like "Area is overrun by the Abyssal").
- 0.5.0: must be **identified**; mod bonuses (Monster Effectiveness, Pack Size, Item Rarity, Monster Rarity, Waystone Drop Chance) now multiplicative with Atlas tree; 30 new endgame areas.

## Precursor Towers & Tablets

- Towers hold Precursor Displacer/Extractor/Refiner/Separator bosses.
- Tablets (incl. Abyss Tablets, "Unforeseen Consequences") inject content into nearby maps; some tablet mods double effects ("Energized Ley Lines").

## Citadels & pinnacles

- **Iron, Stone, Copper Citadels** via Tier 15+ waystones; bosses drop **Crisis Fragments** (Ancient/Weathered/Faded).
- Pinnacle arenas: The Burning Monolith (Arbiter of Ash), The Origin Tower (Arbiter of Divinity, new 0.5.x), Caer Tarth, The Withered Willow, Ruins of Kingsmarch, The Ezomyte Megaliths (10-wave gauntlet).
- No XP gain/loss, unlimited revivals in pinnacle arenas.

## Keys & fragments

- **Reliquary Keys** (Azmeri's, Arbiter's, Olroth's, Tangmazu's, Ritualistic, Trialmaster's, Xesht's, Zarokh's, Twilight) — open Reliquary Vaults.
- **Djinn Barya** (Sekhemas boss), **Inscribed Ultimatum** (Trial of Chaos), Trial Coins, Pinnacle Keys, Vault Keys, Map Fragments.
- **Splinters** (Breach/Simulacrum/Petition/Runic): 1/300 stacks → boss keys.
- **Kulemak's Invitation** → Abyssal Lich unlock ([ascendancies/lich.md](../ascendancies/lich.md)).
