# Misc Items & Systems

> Sources: [poe2db — Items](https://poe2db.tw/us/Items), [poe2db 0.5.0](https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.0)

## Gold

- Vendor currency — vendors, gambling, services (Currency Exchange fees, respecs); not a crafting orb.

## Identify

- **Scroll of Wisdom**; unidentified items drop (waystones forced-identifiable in 0.5.0; unidentified waystones grant +30% waystone drop chance).

## Salvage & disenchantment

- **Salvage Bench**: socketed gear → Artificer's Shards; "Superior" items → quality currency; Uniques have special salvage outcomes.
- **Disenchanting** unwanted magic/rare gear → Transmutation/Regal Shards; Uniques → Chance Shards ([crafting/currency-crafting.md](../crafting/currency-crafting.md)).

## Strongboxes

- Opened with **Cryptic Key** (extra open).

## Incubators

- Attach to items; hatch rewards after N kills.

## Stash

- Dedicated **Currency Tab**, **Fragment Stash Tab** (0.5.0), Map Stash Tab (since 0.1.1).
- .build guide export supported in-game (0.5.0+).

## Other systems

- Hideouts & Hideout Doodads.
- **Relics** (Sekhemas) — scale trial difficulty/rewards.
- Rogue's Marker → Rogue Harbour content.
- Desecration bones / Liquid Emotions as crafting systems — see [crafting/](../crafting/index.md).
