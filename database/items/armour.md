# Armour — Slots & Weight Classes

> Sources: [poe2db — Items](https://poe2db.tw/us/Items), [Fextralife Armor](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Armor), [poe2db 0.5.0](https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.0)

## Slots

- **Helmet, Body Armour, Gloves, Boots** + off-hand **Shields, Bucklers, Foci, Quivers**.

## Weight classes

Str (Armour) · Dex (Evasion) · Int (Energy Shield) · hybrids Str/Dex, Str/Int, Dex/Int, Str/Dex/Int. Per-base names for 0.5.x were not fully catalogued this session — pull from the [poe2db Items index](https://poe2db.tw/us/Items) `(open gap)`.

## Defence layers on armour

- Armour (Str), Evasion (Dex), Energy Shield (Int)
- **Deflect** (reformulated 0.5.0, 95% cap) — see [mechanics/defences.md](../mechanics/defences.md)
- **Runic Ward** (0.5.0, implicit on some bases; applied at the Verisium Anvil)

## Notable armour mechanics (0.5.x)

- **Morior Invictus**: 4 rune sockets with per-socket bonuses.
- **Tabula Rasa**: 6 rune sockets (Lv1).
- Salvaging socketed items at the Salvage Bench progresses toward Artificer's Orbs.
- Smith of Kitava converts a white body armour into a customized artifact — see [ascendancies/smith-of-kitava.md](../ascendancies/smith-of-kitava.md).
- Amazon's Stalking Panther: doubles evasion from Helmet/Gloves/Boots, halves Body Armour.
