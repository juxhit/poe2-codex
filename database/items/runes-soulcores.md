# Runes & Soul Cores — Catalog

> Sources: [Maxroll runes & soul cores](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-and-soul-cores), [poe2db 0.5.0](https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.0), [poe2db — Charms index](https://poe2db.tw/us/Charms). Rules: [mechanics/augments-sockets.md](../mechanics/augments-sockets.md)

## Runes

- Drop from any monster. Tiers **Lesser → Normal → Greater** (3→1 upgrade at Reforging Bench) + non-upgradable **Endgame Runes** (maps; some only in the Ezomyte Megaliths).
- Named campaign runes: Glacial/Desert/Storm (elemental res up to +18%), Iron Rune (phys damage / core defences), Body (+30/45/60 Life), Mind (+20/30/40 Mana), Rebirth, Inspiration, Stone, Vision, Robust/Adept/Resolve (+6/9/12 attributes), Thane Leld/Myrk/Hestra (elemental damage vs Shocked/Ignited/Chilled-Frozen enemies).
- 0.5.0 additions: **13 Ancient Runes** (weapon-type-specific, Remnants post-Act 4), **13 Mythical Runes** (level 15+), 15 meta-crafting runes, 15+ Runic Ward runes, **60+ runes made by destroying a Unique** (inherit properties).

## Soul Cores (Vaal)

- Only from the **Trial of Chaos**; most **Limited: 1 per character**.
- Examples: Soul Core of Ticaba (+12% Critical Damage Bonus, weapon), Atmohua's Soul Core of Retreat (ailment & stun threshold = 15% of max ES), Soul Core of Azcapa (Spirit + Item Rarity), Atziri's Soul Core of Devotion, Jiquani's Soul Core of Rippling, Raven-Touched Shard.
- Complete effect table `(open gap)` — pull from [poe2db /us/Augment](https://poe2db.tw/Augment).
- **Core Destabiliser** (Atziri's Temple) can create **Ancient Soul Cores** (may destroy the core).

## Abyssal Eyes (Ancient Augments)

- From Abyssal Depths (area level 79+); shared limit **1 Ancient Augment per character**.
- **Ulaman's Gaze** — helmet: +1 accuracy per 1 evasion; gloves: lucky crits vs parried enemies; body: prevent 3% of deflected damage.
- **Amanamu's Gaze** — helmet: removes a damaging ailment on command skill use; body: +2 armour per 1 Spirit; boots: up to 40% move speed from Spirit.

## Socketing (permanent)

- Replacing an Augment destroys the old one; **Orb of Extraction** retrieves it but destroys the gear ([crafting/augments.md](../crafting/augments.md)).
