## Notes

- No PoE1-style link sockets: supports socket into gems ([crafting/currency-crafting.md](../crafting/currency-crafting.md)).
- Rune sockets: body armour & two-handers up to 2; one-handers/helmets/gloves/boots 1 ([mechanics/augments-sockets.md](../mechanics/augments-sockets.md)).
- Weapon sets I/II with weapon-set-specific passives; larger weapons swap slower.
- Flails = Templar's presumed weapon; Daggers/Traps = Shadow (1.0) — see [classes/future-classes.md](../classes/future-classes.md).
