# Jewels — Catalog

> Sources: [poe2db — Jewels](https://poe2db.tw/us/Jewels), [0.5.0 notes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-0-patch-notes-return-of-the-ancients). Crafting: [crafting/jewels.md](../crafting/jewels.md)

## Basic jewels

Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire, Diamond — socket into passive-tree jewel sockets. 0.5.0: Ruby can roll +1–2 Maximum Rage; Sapphire 7–13% Chaos Damage.

## Time-Lost Jewels

- Time-Lost Ruby/Emerald/Sapphire/Diamond (radius ~1000 effects) + **Timeless Jewel** base.
- Timeless variants seen in 0.5.x: **Heroic Tragedy** ("Conquered by the Kalguur" / Vorana line), **Undying Hate** ("Conquered by the Abyssals" / Amanamu tribute). Full variant list `(open gap)` — Authority/Restraint-style variants unconfirmed.
- Sources: Time-Lost Cache (Sekhemas), world drops, 10 Ancient Liquid Emotions.

## Sinister Jewel Sockets (via Voices)

- Disconnected from the tree; radius jewels inert; no uniques allowed.

## Special jewel interactions

- **Crystalline Phylactery** (Lich): non-unique basic jewel at 100% increased effect, +50% mana cost, no ES — see [ascendancies/lich.md](../ascendancies/lich.md).
- **Baryanic Leylines** (Disciple of Varashta): +40% radius on non-unique Time-Lost Jewels.
- Crafting: Liquid/Ancient Emotions, Preserved Cranium desecration (32 Desecrated jewel mods), 12 jewel catalysts (0.5.0).
