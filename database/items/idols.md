# Idols — Catalog

> Sources: [Maxroll runes & soul cores](https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.0), [poe2db 0.5.0](https://poe2db.tw/us/Version_0.5.0), [poe2db — Charms index](https://poe2db.tw/us/Charms). Acquisition rules: [crafting/idols.md](../crafting/idols.md)

- Formerly "Talismans"; drop from **Azmerian Wisp**-possessed monsters (Hunting Grounds onward); slot-specific, most **Limited: 1 per character**.
- Datamines referenced ~22 idols pre-0.5.0; four size classes `(unconfirmed — not verified this session)`.
- 0.5.0: **8 new idols** from Azmerian Spirits; **all idols can socket into Sceptres**.
