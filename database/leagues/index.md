# Leagues / Patch History

> Timeline of PoE 2 versions. Full 1.0 details: [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md).

| Patch | Name / Event | Date | Headline features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.1.0** | Early Access launch | **Dec 6, 2024** | Paid EA ($30+ packs); 6 classes (Warrior, Sorceress, Ranger, Monk, Witch, Mercenary), 12 ascendancies; Acts 1–3; Atlas/waystone endgame |
| 0.1.0a–e | Hotfixes | Dec 7–20, 2024 | Crash fixes; campaign boss currency-drop nerfs after first kill |
| **0.1.1** (+a–d) | Endgame/balance | ~Jan 17, 2025 (d: Feb 16, 2025) | 18 pages of notes; tower maps, area checkpoints, 5 respawn attempts vs Arbiter of Ash; Map Stash Tab enabled |
| **0.2.0** | **Dawn of the Hunt** | **Apr 4, 2025** (ended Aug 26, 2025) | **Huntress** class (Amazon, Ritualist); **Abyss** league; ~100 new support gems; sweeping reworks |
| **0.3.0** | **The Third Edict** | **Aug 29, 2025** (ended Dec 8, 2025) | **Act 4** (Ngamakanui, 8 non-linear islands); **3 Interludes** replace Cruel difficulty; **async trade** via NPC Ange; sprinting; support gem overhaul. New ascendancies: Smith of Kitava, Tactician, Lich (+ Abyssal Lich variant) |
| **0.4.0** (+a–e) | **The Last of the Druids** | **Dec 12, 2025** (revealed Dec 4; free weekend Dec 12–15) | **Druid** class (bear/werewolf/wyvern shapeshifting; Talisman lets any class shapeshift); **Shaman**, **Oracle** ascendancies; **Fate of the Vaal** league (player-built dungeons); Abyss core |
| **0.5.0** | **Return of the Ancients** | **May 29, 2026** (runs to Dec 11, 2026) | **Runes of Aldur** league (Verisium crater; Runeforging with Remnants, 150 Runes, Runic Alloys); 30+ new monsters; new pinnacles; **Atlas Passive Tree rework** ("The Endgame Renewed"); Expedition improvements. New ascendancies: **Spirit Walker** (Huntress), **Martial Artist** (Monk), **Disciple of Varashta** (Sorceress, actually 0.4.0-era) |
| **0.5.5** | **Forbidden Rites** (event) | **Sep 4, 2026** | Fresh economy event; Rituals in every zone; **Trial of Chaos overhaul**; Wildwood content; 8 challenges with exclusive rewards; announced with the Duelist deep dive |
| **1.0** | **Full Release** | **Dec 11, 2026** | F2P; Acts 5–6 (Oriath); **Duelist + Shadow**; **Conquerors of Wraeclast** league; faction castle sieges; passive/Atlas rework; 10 classes / 26 ascendancies |

## Notes

- No standalone 0.4.1/0.4.2 or 0.1.2/0.1.3 patches — GGG ships lettered hotfixes (0.4.0a–e, 0.1.1a–d).
- Ascendancy additions timeline: 0.1.0 → 12 · 0.2.0 → 14 (+Amazon, Ritualist) · 0.3.0 → 17 (+Smith of Kitava, Tactician, Lich + Abyssal variant) · 0.4.0 → 20 (+Shaman, Oracle, Disciple of Varashta) · 0.5.0 → 22 (+Spirit Walker, Martial Artist) · 1.0 → 26 (+Duelist/Shadow ascendancies).
- PoE 1 continues side-by-side: Covens of Umbra (3.30) launched free Nov 13, 2026.
