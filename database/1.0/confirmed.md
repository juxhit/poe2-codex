# Path of Exile 2 — 1.0 Full Release (CONFIRMED facts)

> Compiled 2026-11-17. All items verified against official GGG announcements or multiple independent press sources. Rumors/leaks live in [rumors.md](rumors.md).

- **Release date:** **December 11, 2026 (PST)**, ending Early Access. Full reveal at **ExileCon 2026, November 6, 2026** (teaser tagline: "Everything has a price… even fate.").
- **Free-to-play:** PC (Steam, Epic, standalone), PS5, Xbox Series X|S. Monetization shifts to optional cosmetics + stash tabs (PoE 1 model). Steam Deck Verified at 1.0; Mac support for Apple M2-series+ and M1 Pro.
- **Sources:** [Official announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3999366) · [pathofexile2.com/fullrelease](https://pathofexile2.com/fullrelease) · [Maxroll "Everything New In PoE2 1.0"](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/everything-new-in-path-of-exile-2-1-0)

## Campaign — Acts 5 & 6

- Completes the six-act campaign, set in/around **Oriath** (Twilight Order and the Beast as threats; Act 5 opens a gate for the Wraeclast army to invade).
- **24 new areas**, **18 new multi-phase bosses**, 20+ champion-tier rare encounters (press text cites 70+ rare encounters total).

## New league — "Conquerors of Wraeclast"

- Recruit armies (rescue Varek in Clearfell), plan battles on a battle board and fight alongside them.
- Armies consume **Supplies**; troops hired with Gold; rare monsters grant troop types.
- **Commanders** (e.g., Athela) can clear Atlas maps hands-off with reserve troops.
- **Treasury rooms** feed a **Reforging/Runeforging** bench that rerolls "Forgemarked" mods into **permanently fractured** modifiers (incl. "blood infused" effects).

## Faction endgame

- Pick one of **three factions: Karui, Ezomyte, Maraketh**; build a growing castle/stronghold.
- Siege ~100 contested castles across the Atlas for server-wide territory.
- Held territory grants Item Rarity buffs, special maps (Vampire Wisp, monster-merging), Hunting Grounds.
- New endgame story: **the Blood Lord and the Sanguar bloodline**.

## Items, skills, passive tree

- 40+ new Uniques, 70+ reworked Uniques, 200+ new base types, 60+ new active skills.
- Support gem rebalance: Chain no longer penalizes first hit; Penetration merged into blanket elemental penetration; gems adapt to the supported skill.
- Highlights: Ashcaller, Siegebreaker, Windripper. New skills: **Molten Forge** (Warrior), **Soul Extraction** (Witch).
- **Passive tree overhaul:** Jewel Sockets move inside clusters (~18 highway sockets → ~60, some linking separate clusters); new Keystone **Withering Disintegration**.
- **Atlas tree reforged:** condensed to ~70 impactful multi-choice nodes (~50 points by pinnacle).
- 320+ skills, 400+ Uniques, 150+ maps total at 1.0 (press figures).

## Reworks & QoL

- **Mercenary** — background ammo reloading.
- **Infernalist** — spell-casting demon minion, three Infernal Hounds.
- **Blood Mage** — rework.
- **Acolyte of Chayula** — Darkness becomes a Keystone; quarterstaff companion.
- **Death Recap** added; up to **25% CPU performance gains**.

## Totals at 1.0

**10 classes, 26 ascendancies** (long-term: 12 classes / 36 ascendancies via ~4-month post-release cadence).

## Pricing / supporter packs

- EA Supporter Packs (from $30 incl. 300 points + EA key) retired from the store; "early access" no longer applies. ([Official](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4011562))
- Nothing purchased is lost: cosmetics carry over permanently (many usable in both PoE 1 and PoE 2); unspent points stay on the shared wallet.
- 5 new Core Supporter Packs (listed Nov 7): **Archon of Rebirth $60, Ash $100, Destruction $160, Divinity $240, Corruption $480** — upgradable; a $30/300-point option exists. GGG: supporter pack sales fund almost all development.

## GGG statements on 1.0 scope

- **Jonathan Rogers (Game Director), Ungeek interview (Nov 2026):** 1.0 completes the main story (Acts 5–6) and opens the live-service phase after ~8 years of development. Passive tree rework shipped because "the builds were all the same, just allocating jewel sockets" — pulled forward from 0.5.5: "I didn't want to go into 1.0 without it." A **Trial of the Sekhemas upgrade did not make the cut** ("not really up to the standard of the rest of the game"), planned "fairly shortly after launch." ([Ungeek](https://www.ungeek.ph/2026/11/interview-path-of-exile-2-director-jonathan-rogers-on-version-1-0s-big-changes-and-what-reworks-didnt-make-the-cut))
- **Eurogamer (earlier):** Rogers confirmed 1.0 might launch **without all promised classes** (prioritizing campaign/endgame) — Duelist + Shadow did make it; Marauder/Templar did not. ([Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/path-of-exile-2-full-release-classes))
- PoE 1 continues side-by-side: **Covens of Umbra (3.30)** launched free November 13, 2026.
