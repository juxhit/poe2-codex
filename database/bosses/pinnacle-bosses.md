## Tactics notes (known)

- **Uber act bosses (Citadels)** — Doryani, Jamanra the Abomination, Count Geonor: campaign-fight mechanics scale up; see [act-bosses.md](act-bosses.md) for base versions; up to 5 respawn attempts (Arbiter access).
- **Jamanra the Abomination** documented Cold-averse; **Arbiter of Ash** weak to Cold / **Arbiter of Divinity** weak to Fire — bring penetration of the weak element.
- **Trialmaster**: ordeals don't apply; rounds 4/7/10 are bosses — see [mechanics/trials.md](../mechanics/trials.md) for Fate bosses (Uxmal/Bahlak/Chetza).
