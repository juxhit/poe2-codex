# Act Bosses — Bestiary & Tactics

> Sources: [Maxroll Act 1–4 + Interludes boss guides](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/act-1-boss-guide). Damage types per Maxroll; resistances rarely documented. Campaign route: [guides/campaign-route.md](../guides/campaign-route.md)

## Act 1

| Boss | Zone | Damage | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bloated Miller | The Riverbank | Phys | Tutorial fight |
| **The Executioner** | Ogham Village | Phys, Fire | Soldiers reinforce — keep clearing adds; "Execution Sentence" red-axe slam is heavy AoE |
| **Count Geonor** (act boss) | Ogham Manor | Phys, Cold | Dodge-roll through Avalanche frost walls; wait out rear frost sweep after Impale; ~25% → **Geonor the Putrid Wolf** (enraged cold). Uber version is a Citadel boss |

## Act 2

| Boss | Zone | Damage | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rathbreaker | Vastiri Outskirts | Phys | <60% summons hyena swarms — kill fast; spear rain follows charge |
| Rudja, the Dread Engineer | Mawdun Mine | Phys, Fire | Oil grenades slow + Fire Exposure; drag her through undetonated explosives to stagger |
| Jamanra, the Risen King | Halani Gates | Phys, Lightning, Fire | Stand in the small circle around his sword during Flameblast; 25% sandstorm + Volatile Scarabs |
| Zalmarath, the Colossus | Titan Grotto | Phys, Fire | Stand behind the dagger during Decimation Beam (phase 2); ~35% destroy flying constructs |
| Tor Gul, the Defiler | Spires of Deshar | Phys, Fire, Chaos | Flame Breath covers a third of the arena; <50% flame runes spawn tornadoes + skulls |
| **Jamanra, the Abomination** (act boss) | Dreadnought Vanguard | Phys, Lightning, Fire | Hide behind Sekhema Asala's shield during Sandstorms while killing zombies; **Cold-averse** |

## Act 3

| Boss | Zone | Damage | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zicoatl, Warden of the Core | Jiquani's Sanctum | Phys, Lightning | Pyramid Slam safe outside triangle corners; roll through or run past Shocking Blast |
| Ketzuli, High Priest of the Sun | Temple of Kopec | Fire | Invulnerable intermissions raining fireballs/black holes, then lands + enrage-dashes; Black Hole Sun slows closer |
| Viper Napuatzi | Utzaal (Past) | Phys, Lightning, Chaos | Shrinking arena + meteor showers = DPS race; dodge-roll *into* poison guard waves |
| **Doryani, Royal Thaumaturge** (act boss) | Black Chambers (Past) | Phys, Lightning, Cold, Fire | 3+ phases; <50% mounts the Doryani's Triumph mech; refracted lightning beams (~120° cones) — never stand in them; circle arena, roll when the Fist slows |

## Act 4

| Boss | Zone | Damage | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Krutog, Lord of Kin | Volcanic Warrens | Phys, Lightning | Exploding corpses overlap; Sulphite puddles; lightning stomp while chasing |
| Yama, the White | Halls of the Dead | Phys, Chaos | Dispel 6 spectral clones on pillars; "Guessing Game" spawns 4 clones of you — move speed helps |
| Torvian, Hand of the Saviour | Arastas | Phys, Fire | Toss boulders at caster platforms to interrupt; arena walls slow you; <25% rotating beam |
| Benedictus, First Herald | The Excavation | Phys, Fire, Lightning | "Earthen Maze" nested rock rings — escape via glowing openings; 50% arena halves |
| **Tavakai, the Chieftain** (act boss) | Heart of the Tribe | Phys, Fire, Cold, Lightning | 3 phases: Chieftain → the Fallen (50%) → the Consumed (25%); Ngamahu fireballs, Kitava mark (his damage heals him — dodge), Tukohama Jade Slam |

## Interludes (highlights)

| Boss | Interlude | Damage | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thane Wulfric & Lady Elyswyth | I1 | Phys, Fire, Chaos | Elswyth's Bone Furnace traps you before a pillar lands; **pull Elswyth solo** — Wulfric enrages |
| Oswin, the Dread Warden | I1 | Phys, Cold | Cold flames stack Profane Flame (crit vs you); edge spike traps freeze; expanding Ice Crash |
| Azmadi, the Faridun Prince | I2 | Phys | Sunder has almost no telegraph — phys mitigation; ~70% hide behind him for Temporal Sunder |
| **Lythara, the Wayward Spear** | I3 | All five types | Serpent's Bite unblockable — dodge sideways; Wildwood Wisps grant her random buffs — fight far away; damage-check (+40 Spirit reward) |
| Zolin & Zelina | I3 | Phys, Cold | Zolin's Blood Pool + Vaal lightning beams; Zelina's Flurry → blood-bear slam |

## Optional / side bosses (one-liners)

- **Act 1:** Beira (wolves + frost runes, +10% Cold Res) · The Devourer (submerge/erupt) · The Brambleghast (Volatile Slam one-shots squishies) · The Rust King · The Rotten Druid (interrupt vine channel) · Asinia (grave knights) · Draven · Lachlann (one-shot Earthquake cone) · Crowbell (+2 points) · King in the Mists (purple ritual move / red ritual stand still, +30 Spirit) · Candlemass (+20 Life)
- **Act 2:** Balbala (hide-and-seek gas — unlocks Sekhemas) · L'im the Impaler · Iktab & Ekbab (kill Iktab first) · Kabala (+2 points, Bone Cage escape) · Azarian (shrinking arena, fire res)
- **Act 3:** Rootdredge (Detonate Dead necromancer) · Mighty Silverfist (lethal Tumble, +2 points) · Xyclucian (break pillar perches) · Blackjaw (+10% Fire Res) · Mektul (4-min flood timer, Reforging Bench) · Queen of Filth (detonate mushrooms)
- **Act 4:** Blind Beast (fissure detonations) · Great White One (sand breaches + piranha pools) · Diamora (Siren Song petrifies) · Captain Hartlin (cannon semicircles) · The Prisoner (ballista counters regen) · Scourge of the Skies (plagueling swarm)
- **Interludes:** Isolde & Heldra · Siora (reach runed megalith) · Sigbert & Godwin (Blast Furnace) · Vornas (hasted flame phase ~60%) · Elzarah (cobras, tornados) · Rakkar (aim snowball into wall) · Abominable Yeti (+2 points) · Stormgore (awakening statues)

## Uber act bosses (Citadels)

Doryani, Jamanra (Abomination), Count Geonor — Uber versions drop **Crisis Fragments**; see [mechanics/pinnacle-bosses.md](../mechanics/pinnacle-bosses.md) and [items/waystones.md](../items/waystones.md).
