# Augments: Runes, Soul Cores & Abyssal Eyes

> Sources: [Maxroll runes & soul cores](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-and-soul-cores), [poe2db — Augment](https://poe2db.tw/Augment), [Fextralife Soul Cores](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Soul_Cores)

**Terminology since 0.4.0:** Socketables → **Augments**; Rune Sockets → **Augment Sockets**; Talismans → **Idols**. Catalog with values: [items/runes-soulcores.md](../items/runes-soulcores.md).

## Augment sockets

- Armour and **two-handed** weapons: up to **2 sockets**; quivers, caster weapons and jewellery: none.
- Added via **Artificer's Orb** (10 shards from salvaging socketed gear = 1 orb).
- Rare high-tier drops can exceed the limit by 1; Vaal Orb can add a socket beyond the cap; Morior Invictus has extra sockets.

## Socketing/removal rules

- Augments socket into empty sockets; **cannot be removed normally** — replacing destroys the old one.
- **Orb of Extraction** retrieves the Augment intact but **destroys the item**.

## Runes (Kalguuran)

- Three campaign tiers — **Lesser, Normal, Greater** (upgrade 3→1 at the Reforging Bench), plus separate **Endgame Runes** from Maps (e.g. Ezomyte Megaliths) that can't be upgraded.
- 0.5.0 retuned values upward (Body Rune +30/45/60 Life) and added: 13 **Ancient Runes** (weapon-type specific), 13 **Mythical Runes**, 15 meta-crafting runes, 15+ Runic Ward runes, 60+ runes made by destroying a Unique. Some alter crafting (Uhtred's Sidereus Rune enables Chronomancy mods).
- Shaman's Wisdom of the Maji consumes **Bonded** modifiers on Runes and Idols.

## Soul Cores (Vaal)

- Drop from the **Trial of Chaos** (~52 cores in 0.5.5); slot-specific effects, most **Limited: 1 per character**.
- Examples: Soul Core of Ticaba (50% reduced Critical Damage taken), Azcapa (Spirit + Item Rarity), Tacati (25% increased Poison Magnitude on martial weapons / +13% Chaos Res on armour).
- **Core Destabiliser** (Atziri's Temple) converts a Soul Core into a stronger **Ancient Soul Core** (shares Ancient Augment limit of 1) but may destroy it.

## Abyssal Eyes (Ancient Augments)

- Ulaman's Gaze / Amanamu's Gaze with helmet/body/glove/boot effects; only **1 Ancient Augment per character total**.
