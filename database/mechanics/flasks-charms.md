# Flasks & Charms

> Sources: [Mobalytics charms](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/charms), [Mobalytics ailments](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/ailments); flask numbers `(unconfirmed)` for 0.5.x

## Flasks

- **Charge-based:** flasks **no longer refill from kills** — they regain charges while fighting (deal/take damage) and fully recharge in town/hideout.
- Typical capacity **60–80 charges, ~10 per use** (e.g. Colossal Life Flask 75 charges).
- Recovery is **over time and continues while moving** (Lesser Life Flask ~470 life/3s; Lesser Mana Flask 50 mana/2s).
- **5 flask slots** per character.
- Upgrades: **Glassblower's Baubles** (quality → capacity), affix rolls via Transmute/Augmentation.
- Pathfinder flask nodes: Enduring Elixirs (effects don't stop at full life), Azmeri Brew (Amazon — flasks recover both), charge gains (Ritualist Mind Phylacteries consumes mana instead).
- A "massive flask rework" was announced for late 0.5.x/1.0 era `(unconfirmed details)`.

## Charms

- Replace PoE 1 utility flasks: up to **3 equipped** belt attachments with their own charges that **auto-trigger** on a condition:

| Charm | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Silver | Slowed |
| Thawing | Frozen |
| Staunching | Bleeding |
| Antidote | Poisoned |
| Dousing | Ignited |
| Grounding | Shocked |

- Charm charge gain comes from gear/passives/idols (Ritualist: Intricate Sigils +1 charm slot, Mind Phylacteries — consume 25% mana to trigger charge-less charms).
- Shaman's Sacred Flow: +40 Spirit per empty charm slot (empty slots become a resource).
