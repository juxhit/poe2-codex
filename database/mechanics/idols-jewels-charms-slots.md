# Idols, Jewels & Special Slots

> Sources: [poe2db — Idol](https://poe2db.tw/us/Idol), [Fextralife Idols](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Idols), [0.5.0 patch notes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-0-patch-notes-return-of-the-ancients)

## Idols

- **Azmeri-fashioned Augments** dropped by Azmerian Spirit-possessed monsters (Hunting Grounds onward; "Those Who Hear" passive: +50% idol drop chance in Lv75+ areas).
- Slot into a dedicated **Idols inventory panel** granting passive bonuses; **also socketable in Sceptres** since 0.5.0.
- Each idol: main modifier + a **Bonded** modifier (active only with Shaman's Wisdom of the Maji).
- Examples: Fox Idol (body armour: other idols also gain Bonded mods), Stag Idol (helmet: projectiles 15% chance to Fork), Rabbit Idol (12% Rarity / Sceptres +15% Spirit), Panther Idol (+10% of Armour applies to Chaos Damage), Idol of Maxarius (+1 Charm Slot), Idol of Grold (50% increased Power counted by Warcries).
- Most idols are **Limited: 1 per character**; replacing a socketed idol **destroys the original**; no reroll currency `(unconfirmed — improved only by recombining duplicates)`.
- Ritual pinnacle Queen in the Mists drops **three corrupted Idols**.
- Spirit Walker's Idolatry scales off idols — see [ascendancies/spirit-walker.md](../ascendancies/spirit-walker.md).

## Jewels

- Socket into the **passive tree** (some into amulets).
- **Basic jewels** (Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Cobalt…): Magic/Rare crafted with the standard currency loop. 0.5.0 tweaks: Ruby can roll +1–2 Maximum Rage; Sapphire 7–13% Chaos Damage.
- **Catalysts** (Genesis Tree): add quality to jewels; 12 new catalysts in 0.5.0.
- **Liquid Emotions** (Delirium): instill notables on amulets; in 0.5.0 also craft mods on jewels like Greater Essences; 3 new Potent Emotions craft exclusive jewel mods.
- **Time-Lost Jewels**: large-radius jewels with powerful passives. Sources: Time-Lost Cache (end of Trial of the Sekhemas), world drops, 10 Ancient Liquid Emotions (0.5.0). Ancient Emotions remove/add mods on Time-Lost Jewels only.
- **Lich's Crystalline Phylactery**: socket a jewel into the ascendancy node itself (+100% effect) — see [ascendancies/lich.md](../ascendancies/lich.md).
- Disciple of Varashta's Baryanic Leylines: +40% radius on non-unique Time-Lost Jewels.

## Charm slots

- Default charm slots on belts; +1 from Ritualist's Intricate Sigils and Idol of Maxarius.
