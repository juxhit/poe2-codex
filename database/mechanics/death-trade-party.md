# Death, Hardcore, Trade & Party

> Sources: [Game8 death penalty](https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/494914), [GGG async trade FAQ](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3828185), [Mobalytics currency exchange](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/currency-exchange), community-measured numbers `(unconfirmed)`

## Death penalty (EA rules)

- **Softcore:** no XP penalty during the campaign; from **~level 65** (map-device areas), death costs **10% of XP toward the current level** — you **cannot de-level**. Controversial; retained through 0.5.x.
- **Hardcore:** permanent death — on dying, the character + inventory **migrate to Standard**; the **HC stash stays in Hardcore** for your next HC character. PvP deaths also delete the HC character. No stash removal on death.
- **SSF** exists as an opt-in checkbox; migrating an SSF character reportedly leaves stash gear/gems behind `(unconfirmed, single source)`.

## Trade

- **Faustus (Currency Exchange):** instant market-rate bulk currency swaps; posting buy/sell orders costs **Gold** (scales with value — ~10g per Alteration shard, ~250g per Divine requested).
- NPCs: **Finn** (Act 1 Clearfell), **Risu** (Act 2), **Alva** (Act 3 + hideout); **Ange** (0.5.5-era exchange/market NPC; exact role `(unconfirmed)`).
- **Ange / Merchant Tabs (0.3.0):** asynchronous item trading — list gear in your hideout Merchant Tabs at your chosen price; **buyers pay the gold fee, sellers pay nothing**.
- **Item-for-item trades:** still via [pathofexile.com/trade2](https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2) website listings with whisper/one-click pickup in-game.
- 1.0: community expects further async-trade movement, GGG historically resists a fully automated auction house — see [1.0/rumors.md](../1.0/rumors.md).

## Party scaling

- Monsters gain roughly **+50–60% more Life per extra player** `(unconfirmed, community-measured; ~4× HP in a 3-player party reported)`.
- Monster damage scaling is disputed/patch-dependent (early EA reports of inflated party damage).
- Vaults of Kamasa unique-map chests are exempt from party bonuses (0.4.0).
- Bosses refill on collective party respawn; respawn count is shared.
