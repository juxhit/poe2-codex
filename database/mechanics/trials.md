## Trial of the Sekhemas

### Entry & Barya tiers

- First access via quest Barya (Act 2, Balbala). **Djinn Barya** drops from any monster Act 2+; harder with area level: 24–44 → 1 floor; 45–59 → 2; 60–74 → 3; **75+ → 4 floors**. Each floor = 7 rooms + boss.
- Floor bosses: Test of Strength (**Rattlecage the Earthbreaker**), Test of Will (**Terracotta Sentinels** — Rafiq of the Frozen Spring & Hadi of the Flaming River), Test of Cunning (**Ashar the Sand Mother**), Test of Time (**Zarokh the Temporal**).
- Completing tests unlocks the 12-slot Relic Inventory.

### Honour system

- Honour pool scales off max Life + ES; every hit costs Honour (Armour reduces loss, Evasion avoids it); **0 Honour = run fails**.
- **Sacred Water** currency (shrines cost 5; restores Honour); Bronze/Silver/Gold keys open matching Caches.
- Shrines: Halani (Honour restore), Ahkeli (big restore + adds Affliction), Orbala (random Boon), Galai (Boon + removes Affliction), Tabana `(unconfirmed)`.

### Relics

- Bases by size: Seal Relic (2), Urn Relic (2), Amphora Relic (3), Tapestry Relic (3), Incense Relic (4), Vase Relic (4), Coffer Relic (4). Persist between trials; craftable to Magic only.
- Affixes — Prefixes: max Life, max Honour, Move Speed, Rare/Boss/normal monsters take increased damage, monsters deal reduced damage, chance for extra Key. Suffixes: Relic drop quantity, Dodge Roll distance, **Honour Resistance** (key stat — target 75%, up to 90%), Key drop quantity, reduced Merchant Prices, additional Rooms revealed, max Honour Resistance.
- Numeric rolls not public `(open gap)`.

| The Peacemaker's Draught (Amphora) | Armour/Evasion/ES = 0 | 1 extra Barya |
| The Desperate Alliance (Vase) | Zarokh deals 100% more / takes 75% less | **Against the Darkness** |

### Boons (48) & Afflictions (58) & Pledges (18)

- **Boons** (bought with Sacred Water / shrines), examples: Sacred Mirror (duplicates keys), Sekhema's Cloak (revive once at 0 Honour), Imperial Seal (disables traps), Raincaller (doubles Sacred Water), Hare Foot (+40% move speed), Ornate Dagger (50% more damage), Sanguine Vial (monsters −30% life), Black Pearl (traps −50% damage), Silver Tongue (−50% merchant prices).
- **Afflictions** (picked via room choice/shrines), examples: Corrosive Concoction (defences zeroed), Unquenched Thirst (blocks Sacred Water), Branded Balbalakh (blocks Honour restore), Deadly Snare (traps ×3 damage), Myriad Aspersions, Purple Smoke, Fiendish Wings, Blunt Sword, Dark Pit, Deceptive Mirror.
- **Pledges** (18, Kochai rooms) — trade-offs: Pledge to the Guileful (one keyless chest per room, take phys damage), Pledge to the Deserted (−50% merchant prices, no Sacred Water drops).
- Full 48/58/18 name lists `(open gap)` — counts from poe2db, examples from Maxroll.

## Trial of Chaos

### Inscribed Ultimatum tiers

- Area 39–59 → **4 rounds**; 60–74 → **7**; **75+ → 10**. Rounds 4/7/10 always boss fights. After round 10 you may wager currency to continue in 10-round blocks (up to 30 total).
- Losing any round forfeits all pending loot. Encounter types: Slay all Monsters, Survive, Implant the Soul Cores, Escort the Statue, Sacrifice Monsters, Destroy the Idols, Slay the Boss.
- Reward chests each round; boss rounds can offer Soul Cores (of Azcapa/Quipolatl most valuable).
- **No positive Boons in PoE2** — you pick 1 of 3 negative **ordeals** before each round, lasting the whole run.
- Wager modifiers (rare): Wager of the Present, Rarity, Rerolling, Ruin, Mystery, Upgrades, Danger, the Future, Chaos.

### Ordeals (with ranks)

Stormcaller Runes (3) · Vaal Omnitect (3) · Stalking Shade (3, Ruin on hit — fail at 7) · Burning Turrets (2) · Shocking Turrets (2) · Pyramid Beams (2, Corrupted Blood) · Impending Doom (2) · Heart Tethers (3) · Reduced Recovery (5, 20–80%) · Petrification Statues (3) · Damaged Defences (5) · Reduced Resistances (5, −15–55%) · Blood Globules (2) · Deadly Monsters (5, crit chance) · Chaotic Monsters (5) · Toxic Monsters (5, bleed/poison) · Time Paradox (3) · Resistant Monsters (5, +10–50% res) · Unstoppable Monsters (3) · Entangling Monsters (Grasping Vines) · Enraged Bosses (3) · Lethal Rare Monsters (3) · Volatile Fiends (5) · Escalating Damage Taken (3, up to +50%) · Lessened Reach (5) · Drought (5, flask/charm charges) · Monster Speed (3) · Shielding Monsters (5) · Prismatic Monsters (5).

Modifiers do **not** affect The Trialmaster.

### Fates & Trialmaster

- Round-10 bosses drop Fates: **Uxmal the Beastlord → Cowardly Fate, Bahlak the Sky Seer → Deadly Fate, Chetza the Feathered Plague → Victorious Fate**; bring all three to open the Trialmaster's tower.
- First Trialmaster kill grants the **4th Ascendancy point**; repeat kills can drop Glimpse of Chaos, Zerphi's Genesis, Mahuxotl's Machination, Hateforge, The Adorned.
- Corrupted ("Special") Inscribed Ultimatums require sacrifices for boosted rewards.
