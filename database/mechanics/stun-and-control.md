# Stun & Crowd Control

> Sources: [Mobalytics stun](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/stun), [poe2db — Heavy Stun](https://poe2db.tw/us/Heavy_Stun), [Mobalytics immobilise](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/immobilise)

## States

- **Immobilised** is the umbrella state = **Heavy Stunned, Pinned, Frozen, or Electrocuted**.
- Several ascendancies scale off immobilising: Martial Artist (Way of the Mountain Teachings), Tactician (Suppressing Fire), Oracle (Immobilisation Buildup node), Titan (Crushing Impacts).

## Stun

- Triggered by damage vs **Stun Threshold**; Physical and Melee damage each deal **50% more Heavy Stun build-up**.
- Titan: +18% Stun Buildup node; Crushing Impacts — 25% more damage vs Heavy Stunned.

## Heavy Stun

- Hits fill an invisible bar (visible under HP once partially filled); at 100% the target is locked out of actions for a few seconds and counts as **Immobilised**.
- Bar decays if not refreshed.
- Player side: your own bar fills when blocking/being hit (stagger meter) — see [defences.md](defences.md).

## Pin

- Projectile/arrow-based immobilise — notably crossbow/grenade skills and Tactician ("Right Where We Want Them": projectile damage builds Pin; pinned enemies cannot act).

## Electrocute

- Lightning lock ailment, 5s — see [ailments.md](ailments.md).

## Freeze

- 100% cold build-up → 4s full lock. Countered by Thawing Charm.
