# Charges & Special Resources

> Sources: [poe2db — Charges](https://poe2db.tw/us/Charges), [Mobalytics charges guide](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/profile/dreamcore/guides/poe-2-charges-explained), [poe2db — Combo](https://poe2db.tw/us/Combo)

## Power / Frenzy / Endurance Charges

- Default max **3 of each**; last **15 seconds**, refreshed by gaining another of the same type.
- **No inherent benefit** — charges are consumed by skills/supports (key design change from PoE 1).

| Charge | Gain examples | Spend examples | Payoffs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Endurance** (Str) | Spending Rage in Bear Form (1 per 15 Rage), Heavy Stuns, fully breaking armour, Magma Barrier blocks | Totems, warcry cooldown bypasses | Life per charge consumed (Kingsguard/Rally), fire damage, +40% armour on sceptres |
| **Frenzy** (Dex) | Sniper's Mark, culls, Parried, Combat Frenzy (on Freeze/Electrocute/Pin), Blood Rage | Spear/bow skills | Skill speed, armour break, +5% movespeed per charge (Darkray Vectors) |
| **Power** (Int) | Power Siphon, crits (Voll's Protector), Killing Palm kills, Siphoning Strike (consumes Shock), Profane Ritual | Flicker Strike, Falling Thunder, Charged Staff | Crit damage bonus per charge (Powertread), more spell damage per charge (Inner Conviction) |

- Conduit/Resonance/Inner Conviction share or convert charge types.

## Combo (Monk)

- **Per-skill counter**, not a global resource: skills **gain Combo when you Strike enemies** and decay if not generated (~4s with Combo Finisher supports; Combo Finisher I = +25% more damage).
- Combo either **required to use** a skill (Tempest Bell, Falling Thunder, Siphoning Strike) or grants extra effects.
- Loop: fast strikes (Tempest Flurry/Ice Strike) build Combo → spend on finishers. Tempest Flurry escalates: 3rd quick use strikes 3×, 4th is a Final Strike; consumes Frenzy Charges for skill speed.
- Martial Artist rewrites the rules: Martial Master (build/retain Combo regardless of weapon set), Martial Adept (+1 per gain) — see [ascendancies/martial-artist.md](../ascendancies/martial-artist.md).

## Rage

- Generic Rage resource (built by melee hits / certain nodes); PoE 2 added **Rage leech**.
- **Druid (0.4.0)**: form-specific resources — **Bear form uses Rage** (mauling hordes; 1 Endurance Charge per 15 Rage spent), **Wolf form uses lunar energy**, **Wyvern form uses Power Charges**; human form casts normally. ([pathofexile2.com/druids](https://pathofexile2.com/druids))
- Shaman scales spell damage off Rage (Druidic Champion: 1% more spell damage per 2 Rage) — see [ascendancies/shaman.md](../ascendancies/shaman.md).

## Glory (Banners)

- Banner skills generate/consume **Glory**. Tactician: banners gain **5 Glory/s**, **no limit** on banners placed.
- Smith of Kitava: Kitavan Imprint +60% Glory generation.
- Banner passives trade Glory for stronger effects.

## Infernal Flame (Infernalist)

- Infernalist skills can **gain Infernal Flame instead of spending Mana**; **25% of Flame is lost per second** if none gained in the last 2 seconds.
- Pyromantic Pact: Mana replaced by 2× Infernal Flame; hitting max Flame deals Life+ES as Fire damage. See [ascendancies/infernalist.md](../ascendancies/infernalist.md).

## Darkness (Acolyte of Chayula)

- Counterpart resource to Infernal Flame (defensive): Embrace the Darkness — no Spirit, base max Darkness 100 (+10/level), damage reserved from Darkness before Life/ES.
- Volatility: gain on inflicting ailments (Inner Turmoil) / on kill (small node) — 1 Volatility triggers speculations; agent could not independently confirm all Volatility details `(unconfirmed)`.
