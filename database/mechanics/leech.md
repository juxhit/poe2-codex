# Leech

> Source: [poe2db — Leech](https://poe2db.tw/us/Leech)

- Leech recovers Life/Mana/ES/**Rage** over a fixed **1 second**, scaled to the hit's damage (% of Physical damage dealt; weapon mods ~5–10%).
- **Only one leech instance per resource can be active** (major change from PoE 1's concurrent instances).
- "(8–15)% of Leech is Instant" exists via Abyss jewel mods.
- Overflow possible via uniques (Couture of Crimson, Soul Tether).
- **Vaal Pact**: 50% more leech amount, 67% less leech rate.
- Ascendancy leech nodes: Vitality Siphon (Blood Mage, 20% of spell damage), Mystic Harvest/Sap of Nightmares (leech from Elemental/Chaos damage).
