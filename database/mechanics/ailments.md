# Ailments

> Sources: [Mobalytics ailments](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/ailments), [poe2db — Damage Contributing to Ailments](https://poe2db.tw/us/Damage_Contributing_to_Ailments)

## Ailment Threshold

- Works like "elemental armour" against ailment application: larger hits relative to threshold → higher chance and larger magnitude.
- For players based on **half of maximum Life**; raised via passives, Act 3 permanent buff, gear (Ailment Threshold).

## Damaging ailments

| Ailment | Duration | Magnitude | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ignite** | 4s | Fire DoT = **20% of the hit's Fire damage** | Chance from fire portion of hit; biggest hit usually sets it |
| **Bleeding** | 5s | Physical DoT = **15% of pre-mitigation phys damage** | **Doubled while target moves** or if Aggravated; bypasses ES; needs explicit infliction source |
| **Poison** | 2s | Chaos DoT = **20% of pre-mitigation phys hit damage** | Bypasses ES; **only one Poison on a target at a time** (Pathfinder's Overwhelming Toxicity doubles the cap) |
| **Chill** | 2s players / 8s monsters | Action-speed slow | Stormweaver can double-chill up to 35% slow |
| **Freeze** | 4s | Full action lock | Applied at 100% cold **build-up** from big cold hits; countered by Thawing Charm |
| **Shock** | 4s players / 8s monsters | **20% increased damage taken** (base magnitude, scales higher) | Stormweaver can double-shock |
| **Electrocute** | 5s | Action lock, interrupts | Applied at 100% electrocute build-up |

## Application rules

- Chance scales with the relevant damage portion vs the target's Ailment Threshold.
- **Shaper of Storms/Winter** (Stormweaver): all hit damage contributes to Shock chance / Chill magnitude.
- Fire res mitigates ignite damage; chaos res mitigates poison.
- Immunities: Crystallised Immunity supports, Heatproofing (Smith of Kitava), CI (poison/bleed immunity).
- Elemental damage contributes to Bleeding magnitude via Blood Barbs (Blood Mage).
