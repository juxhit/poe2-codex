# Atlas & Waystones (Endgame Loop)

> Sources: [Fextralife Endgame Guide](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Endgame_Guide), [Mobalytics Atlas progression](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/endgame-atlas-progression-guide), [official 0.5.0 notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)

!!! tip "Sustain levers"
    Atlas passive nodes · **Waystone Drop Chance** suffix on waystones · precursor tablets · unidentified waystones (+30% drop chance) · bosses drop **+1 tier**.

## Waystones (= maps)

- **Tiers 1–16** (T1 = area level 65 → T16 = 80). Reforge **3 same-tier waystones → 1 higher-tier** waystone at the Reforging Bench.
- Zone gates: **Fortress T6+**, Enigma Chambers T10+, **Citadels T15**.
- Waystones must be **identified** before use (0.5.0); modifiers rebalanced, Monster Rarity modifier added.
- **Unidentified waystones grant +30% Waystone Drop Chance** (drop-bonus mechanic).

## Drop & sustain mechanics (0.2.0 redesign, still current)

- Map bosses drop waystones **+1 tier** (never lower than current).
- Map bosses and non-summoned rares have high waystone drop chance (T1: 100%), scaling down with tier.
- Doryani sells up to **10 T1 waystones**.
- Sustain levers: Atlas nodes, "Waystone Drop Chance" suffix on waystones, precursor tablets.

## Mapping loop

1. Socket waystone → clear map (boss kill completes the map since 0.3).
2. 1–3 bonus features spawn per map (league mechanics, shrines, strongboxes, essences, wisps, rogue exiles, summoning circles).
3. 6 respawn portals per map (reduced by waystone modifiers; party shares; boss refills HP on collective respawn).

## Precursor Tablets & Towers

- Since **0.3.1** tablets socket into the **Map Device** (not towers): **3 tablet slots** per map, unlocked by map modifier count (6 modifiers → all slots).
- Tablets have limited **uses**, destroyed at 0; same-type tablets stack/amplify; Fracturing Orbs and some tablet crafts need Atlas passive specialization.
- **Towers** remain Atlas structures that reveal the map and **drop tablets**; 0.5.0 added 40+ "Ancient Modifiers" and new Precursor Tower maps.

## Atlas Passive Tree & Fortress (0.5.0 "Origins of the Divinity")

- A central **Fortress** rises after your first Tower clear.
- **Atlas passives are now earned by clearing Fortress sections** (T6/T11/T14-gated storyline) instead of purely map completions.
- Tree expanded to **300+ nodes**; 30 new endgame map areas; every league mechanic has a **guided questline** to its pinnacle with guaranteed repeatable non-quest versions.
- **Citadels** (Stone/Copper/Iron): require high-tier/4-modifier waystones; each houses an Act boss dropping a **Crisis Fragment**; three fragments open the **Burning Monolith** (Arbiter of Ash) — inside the Fortress since 0.5.0.
- 1.0 (Dec 11 2026): Atlas tree condensed to ~70 multi-choice nodes, ~50 points by pinnacle — see [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md).
