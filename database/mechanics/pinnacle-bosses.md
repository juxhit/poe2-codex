# Pinnacle Bosses — Access System

> Boss list + how to reach each. Tactics & loot tables: [bosses/pinnacle-bosses.md](../bosses/pinnacle-bosses.md)
> Sources: [Fextralife Endgame Guide](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Endgame_Guide), [Maxroll boss loot cheat sheet](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/boss-loot-table-cheat-sheet), [timesaver pinnacle guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-pinnacle-bosses-guide)

| Boss | League/system | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Xesht, We That Are One** | Breach | Key from Tul & Esh in Breach Domains |
| **Olroth, Origin of the Fall** | Expedition | Level 79+ Logbook / Grand Expedition content |
| **King in the Mists** (+ Bodach; optional **Queen in the Mists**) | Ritual | Sacrifice unspent Tribute for an audience; Head of the King → Rite of the Nameless (5-map chain) |
| **Omniphobia, Fear Manifest** / **Kosis** | Delirium / Simulacrum | Simulacrum 7-wave key |
| **Kulemak** | Abyss | Defeat 3 Lightless bosses in Abyssal Depths → Kulemak's Invitation → Vessel of Kulemak (Well of Souls) |
| **Zarokh, the Temporal** | Trial of the Sekhemas | Depth-scaled keys/relics |
| **The Trialmaster** | Trial of Chaos | Inscribed Ultimatum |
| **Arbiter of Ash** | Atlas act-boss pinnacle (Burning Monolith, in Fortress) | 3 Crisis Fragments (Stone/Copper/Iron Citadels) |
| **Arbiter of Divinity** | NEW 0.5.0 Fortress pinnacle | Origin Core (quest or repeatable non-quest version) |

## Resistances (0.5.5 notes)

- **Arbiter of Divinity**: resists Lightning, vulnerable to Fire.
- **Arbiter of Ash**: resists Fire, vulnerable to Cold.
- Omniphobia and Raven Trickster lost all-elemental resist.
