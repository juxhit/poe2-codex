# Spirit

> Sources: [poe2db — Spirit](https://poe2db.tw/us/Spirit), [RPS character sheet](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/path-of-exile-2-attributes-character-sheet)

**Spirit** is PoE 2's dedicated reservation resource — the replacement for PoE 1's life/mana reservation.

## Rules

- Powers **permanent-effect skills**: auras, reserved buffs, persistent minions, heralds, banners (Tactician), bells (Monk).
- **Not spent per cast** — reserved while the skill is active; unreserving the skill returns the Spirit.
- Managed from the Skills Panel rather than through life/mana costs.

## Sources

- Gear: e.g. +30 Maximum Spirit items, Spirit on sceptres (quality etchers), Time-Lost/cache pieces.
- Passive tree nodes.
- Ascendancy examples: Tribute to Utula (Smith of Kitava +30%), Lead me through Grace (Invoker: Spirit from body armour ES/Evasion, blocks equipment Spirit), Sacred Flow (Shaman: +40 per empty charm slot), Beidat's Will (Infernalist: Spirit from reserved Life), Embrace the Darkness (Acolyte: **no Spirit at all** — replaced by Darkness).
- Idol of Maxarius / Rabbit Idol interactions (Sceptres +15% Spirit per Bonded idol mods).

## Class resources that replace Spirit

- **Acolyte of Chayula**: Darkness pool (Embrace the Darkness keystone) — see [ascendancies/acolyte-of-chayula.md](../ascendancies/acolyte-of-chayula.md).
- **Infernalist** (Pyromantic Pact): Maximum Mana replaced by Infernal Flame — Mana and Spirit remain separate systems; Spirit still reserved normally.
