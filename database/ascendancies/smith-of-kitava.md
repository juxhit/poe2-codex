# Smith of Kitava (Warrior Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Warrior](../classes/warrior.md)
- **Theme:** A blacksmith who forged a pact with Kitava, the insatiable god. Literally forges his own gear: tempering his weapon and hammering raw bonuses into a plain chestplate.
- **Mechanical identity:** Fire/Strength melee + "forging": converts a Normal-rarity Body Armour into a customized artifact via body-armour modifier nodes, plus weapon-tempering skills.
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.3.0)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Smith_of_Kitava](https://poe2db.tw/us/Smith_of_Kitava)
