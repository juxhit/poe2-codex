# Chronomancer (Sorceress Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Sorceress](../classes/sorceress.md)
- **Theme:** A weaver of time itself — she slows foes to a crawl, rewinds her own injuries, and echoes spells across moments.
- **Mechanical identity:** Time manipulation: Temporal Rift (rewind), Time Freeze, Sands of Time, echo/repeat of spells, innate slow aura, delayed-damage defense.
- **Trial:** Any (Chaos historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Chronomancer](https://poe2db.tw/us/Chronomancer)
