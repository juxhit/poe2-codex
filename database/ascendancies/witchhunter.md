# Witchhunter (Mercenary Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Mercenary](../classes/mercenary.md)
- **Theme:** A ruthless inquisitor of the Templar orthodoxy who executes the corrupted — culling the wounded, shattering enemy concentration, and warding himself with ritual sorcery.
- **Mechanical identity:** Culling/execution vs low-life, "Concentration" mechanic (Witchbane/No Mercy), Sorcery Ward defense, corpse explosions, weapon-set passive flexibility.
- **Trial:** Any (Sekhemas historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Witchhunter](https://poe2db.tw/us/Witchhunter)

## Passive nodes (14, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Witchhunter | Ascendancy start node |
| Damage vs Low Life Enemies (×2 nodes) | 35% increased Damage with Hits against Enemies on Low Life each |
| Cooldown Recovery Rate (×2 nodes) | 6% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate each |
| Armour and Evasion | 15% increased Armour and Evasion Rating |
| Area of Effect | 8% increased Area of Effect |
| Pitiless Killer | Grants Culling Strike |
| Judge, Jury, and Executioner | Grants Decimating Strike |
| Witchbane | Enemies' Maximum Concentration equals 30% of their Maximum Life; you break Concentration on Hit equal to damage dealt; regen 10%/s if none lost in 5s |
| No Mercy | Deal up to 40% more Damage to Enemies based on their missing Concentration |
| Obsessive Rituals | Grants Sorcery Ward skill; 50% less Armour and Evasion Rating |
| Ceremonial Ablution | Sorcery Ward's Barrier can also absorb Physical and Chaos damage from hits |
| Zealous Inquisition | 10% chance for killed enemies to explode dealing 100% of max Life as Physical damage; doubled vs Undead and Demons |
| Weapon Master | 100 Passive Skill Points become Weapon Set Skill Points |
