# Disciple of Varashta (Sorceress Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Sorceress](../classes/sorceress.md)
- **Theme:** A sorceress who commands three bound Djinn — Navira, Kelari, and Ruzhan — redemption-bound spirits that fight, deceive, and burn on her behalf. "By your command, they will earn redemption... one duty at a time."
- **Mechanical identity:** Djinn summoner: each Barya node grants a Djinn skill with three follow-up skill nodes; ES/armour hybrid ("Sacred Rituals"), mana-before-life defense, Focus+Staff dual wield, Time-Lost Jewel scaling.
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.4.0)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Disciple_of_Varashta](https://poe2db.tw/us/Disciple_of_Varashta)
