# Oracle (Druid Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Druid](../classes/druid.md)
- **Theme:** The Oracle has perfected Foresight — peering into future timelines to predict enemies, allocate passives from paths not taken, and force destiny into critical certainty.
- **Mechanical identity:** Timeline manipulation — Entwined Realities/The Unseen Path (allocate normally unreachable non-keystone passives), Moment of Vulnerability (pull an enemy's "future state" into the present), Align Fate, guaranteed crits (Forced Outcome), crit-mitigation defense (The Lesser Harm), totem support (extra max totems, Heartwood).
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.4.0)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Oracle](https://poe2db.tw/us/Oracle)

## Passive nodes (17, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Oracle | Ascendancy start node |
| Passive Point (×2 nodes) | Grants 1 Passive Skill Point each (one tagged Mastery: Ultimatum) |
| Life and Mana Regeneration Rate | 8% increased Life and Mana Regeneration rate |
| Immobilisation Buildup | 20% increased Immobilisation buildup |
| Spell Damage | 12% increased Spell Damage |
| Totem Cast and Attack Speed | Totems' spells get 5% increased Cast Speed; totem attacks get 5% increased Attack Speed |
| Critical Damage Bonus on You | Hits against you have 12% reduced Critical Damage Bonus |
| Critical Hit Chance | 12% increased Critical Hit Chance |
| Entwined Realities | Non-Keystone passives in Medium Radius of allocated Keystones can be allocated without being connected to your tree |
| The Unseen Path | "Walk the Paths Not Taken" (unlock/allocate exclusive path nodes from other ascendancy branches; per community sources) |
| Harmony Within | Hit damage taken from Mana before Life if current Mana exceeds current Life; 15% less max Life and Mana |
| Converging Paths | Grants Skill: Moment of Vulnerability (on an enemy Primed for Stun/Electrocution/Freeze, creates a Visage of its future state) |
| Fateful Vision | Grants Skill: Align Fate |
| Heartwood | +1 to maximum Summoned Totems; Totems die 6 seconds after their Life reaches 0 |
| The Lesser Harm | Enemy crit chance against you is Unlucky; damage of enemies hitting you is Unlucky |
| Forced Outcome | "Inevitable Critical Hits" (per community sources: converts/guarantees critical hits) |

**Note:** Two node effect texts on poe2db are stub-level ("Walk the Paths Not Taken", "Inevitable Critical Hits"); exact wording flagged as low-confidence. Druid's third ascendancy is not yet revealed (see [1.0/rumors.md](../1.0/rumors.md)).
