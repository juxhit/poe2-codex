# Infernalist (Witch Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Witch](../classes/witch.md)
- **Theme:** A witch who pays in flesh for infernal power — a hellhound at her side, mana transmuted into Infernal Flame, and a demonic form that grows with every sacrifice.
- **Mechanical identity:** Demon Form stacking, Infernal Flame resource (replaces Mana via Pyromantic Pact), Beidat life-reservation trio (Mana/ES/Spirit from Life), hellhound minion, ignite/flammability shaping.
- **Trial:** Any (Chaos historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Infernalist](https://poe2db.tw/us/Infernalist)
- **Note:** Reworked for 1.0 — spell-casting demon minion, three Infernal Hounds (see [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md)).

## Passive nodes (18, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Infernalist | Ascendancy start node |
| Bringer of Flame | Damage from you and Allies in your Presence contributes to Flammability and Ignite Magnitudes |
| Flammability Magnitude | 40% increased Flammability Magnitude |
| Beidat's Gaze | Reserves 25% of Life; +1 to Maximum Mana per 6 Maximum Life |
| Beidat's Hand | Reserves 25% of Life; +1 to Maximum Energy Shield per 8 Maximum Life |
| Beidat's Will | Reserves 25% of Life; +1 to Maximum Spirit per 25 Maximum Life |
| Altered Flesh | 20% of Physical Damage taken as Chaos; 20% each of Lightning and Cold taken as Fire |
| Demonic Possession | Grants skill Demon Form; max demon form stacks 10 |
| Mastered Darkness | Demonflame has no maximum |
| Pyromantic Pact | Maximum Mana replaced by twice as much Maximum Infernal Flame; skills cost Infernal Flame instead of Mana; hitting max Flame deals Life+ES as Fire damage; Flame resets at max and decays 25%/s |
| Grinning Immolation | Dealing a Critical Hit ignites you (Fire DPS = 15% of max Life and ES); grants 50% more Critical Damage Bonus |
| Loyal Hellhound | Grants skill Summon Infernal Hound; number of infernal familiars allowed: 1 |
| Seething Body | Casting a spell on High Infernal Flame grants Elemental Archon; persists while on High Flame, lost at maximum Flame |

### Small nodes
- Life — 3% increased maximum Life (multiple nodes)
- Minion Life — 12% increased Minion Life
- Mana — 3% increased maximum Mana
- Spell Damage (×2) — 12% increased Spell Damage each
- Critical Chance — 12% increased Critical Hit Chance
