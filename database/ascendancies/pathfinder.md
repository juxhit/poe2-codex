# Pathfinder (Ranger Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Ranger](../classes/ranger.md)
- **Theme:** A jungle alchemist who weaponises her own flasks — hurling volatile concoctions, stacking poisons beyond mortal limits, and outrunning anything that slows her.
- **Mechanical identity:** Flask/Charges economy (Enduring Elixirs), five granted Concoction throw skills (Explosive/Shattering/Fulminating/Acidic/Bleeding), double poison stack cap, cross-class passive access (Warrior/Sorceress starts), Traveller's Wisdom attribute-flex.
- **Trial:** Any (Chaos historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Pathfinder](https://poe2db.tw/us/Pathfinder)
