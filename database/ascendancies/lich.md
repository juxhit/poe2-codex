# Lich (Witch Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Witch](../classes/witch.md)
- **Theme:** Death itself made manifest — a soul bound to a Crystalline Phylactery who trades life and mana for eternal curse-laden dominion.
- **Mechanical identity:** Curses (infinite duration, +1 curse), ES-immortality (Eternal Life), mana-based Unholy Might conduit, Power Charge spell payoffs, jewel-in-phylactery scaling.
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.3.0; Chaos historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Lich](https://poe2db.tw/us/Lich)

## Passive nodes (15, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lich | Ascendancy start node |
| Curse Area | 15% increased Area of Effect of Curses |
| Mana | 3% increased maximum Mana |
| Life | 3% increased maximum Life |
| Energy Shield | 20% increased maximum Energy Shield |
| Energy Shield if Consumed Power Charge | 30% increased maximum Energy Shield if you've consumed a Power Charge Recently |
| Crystalline Phylactery | Socket a non-Unique Basic Jewel into the Phylactery; 100% increased Effect of Socketed Jewel bonuses; 50% more Mana Cost of skills with no Energy Shield |
| Rupture the Soul | Cursed Enemies killed by you or Allies in your Presence have 33% chance to explode, dealing a quarter of their max Life as Chaos damage |
| Necromantic Conduit | Grants Unholy Might to you and Allies in your Presence while not on Low Mana; Lose 5% of maximum Mana per Second |
| Blackened Heart | 4% increased Magnitude of Unholy Might Buffs you grant per 100 maximum Mana |
| Soulless Form | 10% of Damage taken bypasses Energy Shield; no inherent Mana Regeneration; regenerate Mana equal to 6% of maximum Life per second |
| Eternal Life | Your Life cannot change while you have Energy Shield |
| Price of Power | Spells consume a Power Charge if able to deal 40% more Damage |
| Eldritch Empowerment | Sacrificing Energy Shield doesn't interrupt Recharge; Sacrifice 5% of maximum ES when casting a Spell; Spells that fully paid this Sacrifice deal 30% more Damage |
| Incessant Cacophony | Curses you inflict have infinite Duration; You can apply an additional Curse |

## Abyssal Lich (variant)

- **Unlock:** Not a trial. Bring **Kulemak's Invitation** to the **Well of Souls**, enter the **Black Cathedral**, and interact with the urn ("Embrace Abyssal Power?") — allows free swapping between Lich and Abyssal Lich. Tied to the Abyss mechanic (0.3.0 "The Third Edict").
- **Flavor:** "The Well of Souls is yours to command."
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Abyssal_Lich](https://poe2db.tw/us/Abyssal_Lich) · [Mobalytics guide](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/abyssal-lich)
