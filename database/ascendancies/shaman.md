# Shaman (Druid Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Druid](../classes/druid.md)
- **Theme:** A nature savant who channels elemental fury through Rage — adapting to incoming elements and calling down apocalyptic storms as Glory builds.
- **Mechanical identity:** Rage-as-spell-resource (Furious Wellspring: no inherent Rage loss, mana-regen→Rage, skills cost Rage; Druidic Champion: Rage→more Spell damage), elemental Adaptation defense stack, Exposure aura + random extra elemental damage, Apocalypse glory skill, Charm-slot Spirit (Sacred Flow), Bonded rune/idol modifiers (Wisdom of the Maji).
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.4.0)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Shaman](https://poe2db.tw/us/Shaman)

## Passive nodes (14, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Shaman | Ascendancy start node |
| Elemental Damage | 12% increased Elemental Damage |
| Maximum Rage | +3 to Maximum Rage |
| Elemental Resistances | +3% to all Elemental Resistances |
| Flask Recovery | 12% increased Life and Mana Recovery from Flasks |
| Defences | 10% increased Armour, Evasion and Energy Shield |
| Turning of the Seasons | Enemies in your Presence have Exposure (scaled by rarity: −20% normals to −10% uniques); you gain 10% of Damage as extra damage of a random element |
| Bringer of the Apocalypse | Grants Skill: Apocalypse (triggers apocalyptic elemental spells around you; builds Glory from elemental hits) |
| Druidic Champion | Every 2 Rage grants 1% more Spell damage |
| Reactive Growth | 10% less Elemental Damage taken; adapt once per hit to the highest elemental damage type taken; 10% less damage of each type per matching Adaptation; base max 3 Adaptations |
| Avatar of Evolution | 5% of Physical damage taken as each of Fire/Lightning/Cold; Adaptations last 5 seconds; doubled Adaptation effect |
| Furious Wellspring | No inherent Rage loss; regenerate 6% of max Rage per second; mana regen modifiers apply to Rage regen; skills cost +5 Rage; +7 to Maximum Rage |
| Sacred Flow | +40 Spirit per empty Charm slot |
| Wisdom of the Maji | Gain benefits of Bonded modifiers on Runes and Idols |
