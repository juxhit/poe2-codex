# Spirit Walker (Huntress Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Huntress](../classes/huntress.md)
- **Theme:** Bound to the wilds, she calls upon Azmeri animal spirits — the Stag, the Owl, and the Bear — to stampede, bless, and fight at her side, and can bind even unique beasts in spirit.
- **Mechanical identity:** Three spirit pillars — Vivid Stampede (stag wisps/stampedes on attack), Primal Bounty (owl feathers spent by dodging), Wild Protector (bear spirit companion that doesn't occupy the companion slot) — all three unlock Sacred Unity; plus The Natural Order (tame unique beasts possessed by cycling Azmeri spirits) and Idolatry (idol-scaling companion build).
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.5.0; no binding documented)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Spirit_Walker](https://poe2db.tw/us/Spirit_Walker)

## Passive nodes (18, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Spirit Walker | Ascendancy start node |
| Vivid Stampede | Grants the Vivid Stampede skill; gain a Vivid Wisp per distance moved (max 3) that trigger the skill on attack |
| The Mórrigan's Guidance | Gain a Vivid Wisp when the stampede ends; Stags get 20% more damage and Shock Magnitude per leap |
| Wild Protector | Grants the Wild Protector skill (Bear Spirit companion) |
| The Catha's Balance | Companions gain added attack damage equal to 60% of your main-hand weapon's damage |
| Primal Bounty | Grants the Primal Bounty skill; gain an Owl Feather every 4 seconds (max 3), expended by dodging |
| The Mhacha's Gift | Dodging can spend up to 3 Owl Feathers, each granting 100% more Empowerment effect; feathers gained 50% faster |
| The Natural Order | Tame Beast can capture Unique Beasts (one allowed); they gain 30% movement speed and are possessed by random Azmeri Spirits cycling every 20 seconds |
| Idolatry | Companions deal 10% more damage and you gain 2% Reservation Efficiency per Idol equipped; −4% elemental resistances per non-Idol Augment |
| Sacred Unity | Free to allocate if you have Wild Protector, Primal Bounty, and Vivid Stampede; Bear Spirit gains Embrace of the Wild (regen + damage redirection), Stags leap at enemies, Empowered projectiles leave Soaring Ground (30% evasion, 40% damage on Full Life) |
| Spirit | +10 Spirit |
| Companion Life and Area | Companions: 10% AoE, 15% max Life |
| Shared Companion Damage (×2 nodes) | Companions deal 10% more damage; you gain 10% damage while a Companion is in your Presence |
| Critical Chance (×2 nodes) | 12% increased Critical Hit Chance each |
| Movement Speed (×2 nodes) | 2% increased Movement Speed each |

**Note:** Fextralife describes Owl feathers as adding projectiles/speed — likely earlier 0.5.0-reveal wording; poe2db's dodge-empowerment wording treated as current.
