# Warbringer (Warrior Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Warrior](../classes/warrior.md)
- **Theme:** A Karui war-leader who channels ancestor spirits, wearing enemies down through armour destruction, corpse detonation, and persistent totems.
- **Mechanical identity:** Armour Break as a damage amplifier, warcries that explode corpses, totem support + defensive totem synergy.
- **Trial:** Any (Chaos historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Warbringer](https://poe2db.tw/us/Warbringer)

## Passive nodes (14, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Warbringer | Ascendancy start node |
| Armour Break | Break 25% increased Armour |
| Armour | 20% increased Armour |
| Warcry Speed | 20% increased Warcry Speed |
| Totem Life | 20% increased Totem Life |
| Block Chance | 6% increased Block chance |
| Anvil's Weight | Break Armour equal to 10% of hit damage dealt |
| Imploding Impacts | Fully Broken Armour inflicted increases all damage taken from hits; can Break Enemy Armour below 0 |
| Jade Heritage | Grants skill Encase in Jade; gain a Jade stack per second, 1% additional Physical Damage Reduction per Jade, max 10 stacks |
| Warcaller's Bellow | Warcries Explode Corpses dealing 25% of their Life as Physical Damage; ignore Warcry cooldowns |
| Answered Call | Grants skill Ancestral Spirits; +1 to maximum Summoned Totems; triggers Ancestral Spirits when you summon a Totem |
| Wooden Wall | 20% of damage from hits is taken from your nearest Totem's Life before you |
| Renly's Training | Gain 35% base chance to Block from an equipped Shield instead of the Shield's value |
| Turtle Charm | Take 20% of damage from Blocked Hits; maximum Block chance is 75% |
