# Stormweaver (Sorceress Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Sorceress](../classes/sorceress.md)
- **Theme:** A mistress of the tempest whose every spell crackles with shock and chill, raining elemental consequences on anything that draws her ire.
- **Mechanical identity:** Shock/chill shaper (all damage contributes; double shock/chill application), Arcane Surge uptime, Elemental Storm on spell crits, extra skill limits.
- **Trial:** Any (Sekhemas historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Stormweaver](https://poe2db.tw/us/Stormweaver)

## Passive nodes (16, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Stormweaver | Ascendancy start node |
| Mana Regeneration (×2 nodes) | 12% increased Mana Regeneration Rate each |
| Spell Critical Chance (×2 nodes) | 12% increased Spell Critical Hit Chance each |
| Shock Chance (×2 nodes) | 20% increased chance to Shock each |
| Chill Duration (×2 nodes) | 25% increased Chill Duration on Enemies each |
| Remnant Range (×2 nodes) | Remnants collected from 25% further away each |
| Constant Gale | You have Arcane Surge |
| Refracted Infusion | Collecting an Elemental Infusion grants another, different Elemental Infusion |
| Force of Will | 20% of Damage taken from Mana before Life; Arcane Surge effect increased 20% per 10% missing Mana |
| Tempest Caller | Grants skill Elemental Storm; triggered on Spell Critical Hits |
| Multiplying Squalls | +2 to Limit for Elemental Skills |
| Strike Twice | Targets can be affected by two of your Shocks at once; Shocks deal 25% less Magnitude |
| Heavy Snows | Targets can be affected by two Chills at once; Chills can Slow up to 35%; 25% less Chill Magnitude |
| Shaper of Storms | All Hit Damage contributes to Shock Chance |
| Shaper of Winter | All Hit Damage contributes to Chill Magnitude |
| Storm's Recollection | Remnants collected from 50% further away; collected Remnants reappear once after 3 seconds |
