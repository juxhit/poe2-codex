# Blood Mage (Witch Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Witch](../classes/witch.md)
- **Theme:** A hemomancer who treats her own veins as a mana well — spending life instead of mana, aggravating bleeding wounds, and turning every drop spilled into damage.
- **Mechanical identity:** Life-as-resource (Sanguimancy life costs + Life Remnants), life-crit scaling (Gore Spike), delayed damage (Grasping Wounds), flask charges from life spent (Sanguine Tides), Crimson Power ES-to-Life conversion.
- **Trial:** Any (Sekhemas historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Blood_Mage](https://poe2db.tw/us/Blood_Mage)
- **Note:** Reworked for 1.0 (see [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md)).

## Passive nodes (16, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Blood Mage | Ascendancy start node |
| Spell Critical Chance | 12% increased Critical Hit Chance for Spells |
| Life | 3% increased maximum Life |
| Gore Spike | 1% increased critical damage bonus per 50 current Life |
| Sunder the Flesh | Sets base spell critical hit chance to 15% |
| Sanguimancy | Grants skill Life Remnants; skills gain a base Life cost matching their base mana cost |
| Blood Barbs | Elemental damage contributes to Bleeding magnitude; bleeding on cursed targets is aggravated |
| Whispers of the Flesh | Cursed targets have 100% reduced life regen and at least 15% of Life Reserved |
| Vitality Siphon | 20% of Spell Damage Leached as Life |
| Grasping Wounds | 25% of life loss from hits is prevented, then lost over 4 seconds |
| Sanguine Tides | Flasks don't recover Life; gain flask charges per 2% Life spent; hitting an enemy at full flask charges consumes 40% of them, granting 1% damage as Physical per charge for 5 seconds |
| Bleed on Critical Chance | 15% chance to inflict Bleeding on Critical Hit |
| Curse Effect | 6% increased Curse Magnitudes |
| Life Leech | 12% increased amount of Life Leeched |
| Life Flasks | 15% increased Life Flask Charges gained |
| Crimson Power | Gain additional maximum Life equal to 100% of the Item Energy Shield on equipped body armour |
