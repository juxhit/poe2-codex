# Amazon (Huntress Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Huntress](../classes/huntress.md)
- **Theme:** A master huntress trained to perfection — precise critical strikes, revealed weaknesses, and attacks empowered by elemental surges.
- **Mechanical identity:** Crit/accuracy overflow (crit from excess chance-to-hit), Weakness revelation vs rares/uniques, Elemental Surge charge system (Lightning/Fire/Cold surges), flask-crossover (Azmeri Brew), evasion rearrangement (Stalking Panther).
- **Trial:** Any (Sekhemas historically cited; Amazon was the 0.2 Sekhemas-tied ascendancy)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Amazon](https://poe2db.tw/us/Amazon)

## Passive nodes (16, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Amazon | Ascendancy start node |
| Accuracy | 12% increased Accuracy Rating |
| Skill Speed | 4% increased Skill Speed |
| Elemental Damage | 12% increased Elemental Damage |
| Flask Recovery | 15% increased Life and Mana Recovery from Flasks |
| Evasion | 20% increased Evasion Rating |
| Life Leech | 12% increased amount of Life Leeched |
| Critical Strike | Chance to Hit with Attacks can exceed 100%; gain extra crit chance equal to 25% of excess chance to hit |
| Penetrate | Attacks with your weapons gain added physical damage equal to 25% of the weapon's Accuracy Rating |
| Predatory Instinct | Reveal Weaknesses against Rare and Unique enemies; 50% more damage vs enemies with an Open Weakness |
| In for the Kill | 20% increased Movement Speed and 40% increased Skill Speed while an enemy with an Open Weakness is in your Presence |
| Surging Avatar | Gain 1 fewer Lightning Surge from triggering Elemental Surge; consuming a Charge triggers Elemental Surge to gain 2 Fire Surges and 2 Cold Surges |
| Elemental Surge | Grants the Elemental Surge skill; consuming a Charge triggers it to gain 3 Lightning Surges |
| Azmeri Brew | Life Flasks also recover Mana; Mana Flasks also recover Life |
| Stalking Panther | Evasion from Helmet, Gloves and Boots is doubled; Evasion from Body Armour is halved |
| Mystic Harvest | Life Leech recovers from your Elemental damage as well as Physical damage |

**Note:** Fextralife lists "Infused Avatar" — current poe2db shows Surging Avatar.
