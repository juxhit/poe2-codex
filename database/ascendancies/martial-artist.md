# Martial Artist (Monk Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Monk](../classes/monk.md)
- **Theme:** After decades of training he has mastered three of the seven legendary Hollow Techniques, fighting with pure technique rather than weapons. Creates illusory clones and resonating spirit bells that shatter with his strikes.
- **Mechanical identity:** Combo manipulation (build/retain Combo across weapon sets), three granted "Hollow Technique" skills (Form = clone illusions, Focus = placeable bells, Resonance = crit-ringing back-bell), Runic Meridians extra rune sockets, glove-transformation (Fists of Stone), Way of the Mountain "Teachings" stacking buff.
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.5.0; per-ascendancy binding not documented)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Martial_Artist](https://poe2db.tw/us/Martial_Artist)

## Passive nodes (15, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Martial Artist | Ascendancy start node |
| Attack Speed (×2 nodes) | 4% increased Attack Speed each |
| Evasion and Energy Shield | 15% increased Evasion and 15% increased maximum Energy Shield |
| Area of Effect (×2 nodes) | 8% increased Area of Effect each |
| Additional Power Charge Chance | 10% chance when you gain a Power Charge to gain an additional one |
| Attributes | +5 to all Attributes |
| Immobilisation Buildup | 20% increased Immobilisation buildup |
| Martial Adept | Gain 1 additional Combo when you gain Combo; ES recharge delay −0.2s per Combo expended when using skills |
| Martial Master | Skills can build and retain Combo regardless of Weapon Set; gain Combo from all Attack Hits |
| Hollow Focus Technique | Grants Skill: Hollow Focus (manifest spirit bells around you that can be shattered by attacking) |
| Hollow Resonance Technique | Grants Skill: Hollow Resonance (a bell on your back rings on Critical Hits, damaging nearby enemies) |
| Hollow Form Technique | Grants Skill: Hollow Form (illusions of yourself attack with a chosen skill) |
| Runic Meridians | Grants additional Rune-only sockets: 1 Helmet, 2 Body Armour, 1 Gloves, 1 Boots |
| Way of the Mountain | Gain Mountain's Teachings on immobilising enemies (scales with enemy power, max 30); Teachings lost when Hit or after 8s without gaining; effects include 15% more attack damage, 30% life-threshold damage reduction, 40% less damage taken, 50% more Stun Threshold |
| Way of the Stonefist | Equipped gloves transformed to Fists of Stone base type with upgraded related modifiers; ignore glove Attribute requirements |
