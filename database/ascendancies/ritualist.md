# Ritualist (Huntress Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Huntress](../classes/huntress.md)
- **Theme:** A dark practitioner of Azmeri traditions — blood sacrifice, corrupted lifeforce, and whispers that demand offerings.
- **Mechanical identity:** Ring/Amulet scaling (Mystic Attunement) with extra ring slot, extra charm slot + charge-less charm triggering, Ritual Sacrifice (headhunting without a corpse), Blood Boil corrupted lifeforce, low-life recovery scaling.
- **Trial:** Any (Chaos historically cited; Ritualist was the 0.2 Chaos-tied ascendancy)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Ritualist](https://poe2db.tw/us/Ritualist)
