# Invoker (Monk Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Monk](../classes/monk.md)
- **Theme:** An elemental mystic who channels storm, blizzard, and rage through martial discipline, pairing melee with triggered elemental expressions and meditation.
- **Mechanical identity:** Elemental melee ("I am the Thunder/Blizzard" damage conversions), crit synergy, Meditate, Unbound Avatar, meta-skill/Reservation support.
- **Trial:** Any (Sekhemas historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Invoker](https://poe2db.tw/us/Invoker)
