# Tactician (Mercenary Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Mercenary](../classes/mercenary.md)
- **Theme:** A former Blackguard Elite soldier who runs the battlefield like an officer — banners, supporting fire, defensive emplacements, and buffs that make his whole presence stronger.
- **Mechanical identity:** Banner spam (multiple banners, Glory generation), Presence-based ally buffs, totem/ballista artillery, Pin build-up from projectiles, Deflection/cover defenses.
- **Trial:** Any (added 0.3.0)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Tactician](https://poe2db.tw/us/Tactician)

## Passive nodes (18, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Tactician | Ascendancy start node |
| Spirit | 8% increased Spirit |
| Banner Area | Banner Skills have 16% increased Area of Effect |
| Presence Area | 20% increased Presence Area of Effect |
| Pin Buildup | 20% increased Pin Buildup |
| Armour and Evasion | 15% increased Armour and Evasion Rating |
| Totem Damage | 20% increased Totem Damage |
| Minion Damage | Minions deal 20% increased Damage |
| A Solid Plan | Persistent Buffs have 50% less Reservation |
| Watch How I Do It | Allies in your Presence gain added Attack Damage equal to 25% of your main hand weapon's damage |

| Suppressing Fire | 40% more Immobilisation buildup |
| Right Where We Want Them | Projectile Damage builds Pin; pinned enemies cannot act |
| Polish That Gear | Deflection Rating equal to 20% of Armour; 100% of Evasion gained as extra Ailment Threshold |

| Cannons, Ready! | +1 max Summoned Totems; 30% more Skill Speed for totem skills; totems only attack when you fire an Attack Projectile |
| Unleash Hell! | Grants the skill Supporting Fire |
| Strategic Embankments | Totems you place grant Embankment Auras |
