# Acolyte of Chayula (Monk Ascendancy)

- **Class:** [Monk](../classes/monk.md)
- **Theme:** A monk who surrendered body and mind to Chayula, the Breach darkness — trading Spirit for a dreamlike second pool of "Darkness" that absorbs damage.
- **Mechanical identity:** Darkness resource pool (replaces Spirit via Embrace the Darkness), chaos damage/resistance scaling, Volatility, Remnants, breach-themed skills.
- **Trial:** Any (Chaos historically cited)
- **Source:** [poe2db.tw/us/Acolyte_of_Chayula](https://poe2db.tw/us/Acolyte_of_Chayula)
- **Note:** Reworked for 1.0 — Darkness becomes a Keystone; gains a quarterstaff companion (see [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md)).

## Passive nodes (21, 0.5.x)

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lucid Dreaming | Ascendancy start node |
| Inner Turmoil | Gain 1 Volatility on inflicting an Elemental Ailment; Take no Damage from Volatility |
| Unravelling | Grants the Unravelling skill/passive |
| Sap of Nightmares | Leech recovers based on Chaos Damage as well as Physical Damage |
| Chayula's Gift | +10% Maximum Chaos Resistance; Chaos Resistance is doubled |
| Waking Dream | Grants Skill: Into the Breach |
| Choice of Power | Remnants collectable from 50% further away, 50% increased effect; Flames of Chayula manifest as Purple |
| Choice of Mana | Same Remnant bonuses; Flames manifest as Blue |
| Choice of Life | Same Remnant bonuses; Flames manifest as Red |
| Embrace the Darkness | You have no Spirit; base Maximum Darkness 100 (+10/level); Damage taken is Reserved from Darkness before Life/ES; Darkness reservation lasts 5 seconds |
| Grasp of the Void | Grants Skill: Void Illusion |
| Deepening Shadows | 1% increased maximum Darkness per 1% Chaos Resistance |
| Archon of Chayula | Grants Skill: Archon of Chayula |

### Small nodes
- Volatility — 10% chance to gain Volatility on Kill
- Chaos Damage (×2) — 11% increased Chaos Damage each
- Leech — 11% increased Leech
- Chaos Resistance — +7% to Chaos Resistance
- Skill Speed (×2) — 4% increased Skill Speed each
- Darkness (×2) — +10% maximum Darkness each
- Gain 4% of Damage as extra Chaos Damage
