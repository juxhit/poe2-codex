# Campaign Permanent Rewards Checklist

> Sources: [Maxroll — Permanent Stats From The Campaign](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/permanent-stats-from-campaign), [Maxroll Campaign Guide](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/path-of-exile-2-campaign-guide), [Steam quest-points guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3832896589)

**Campaign totals:** 24 passive skill points (26 incl. endgame), 25 weapon set points, **+100 Spirit**, **+30% elemental res total** (10% per act × 3), +20 Life, +5% max Life and Mana, +5% max Mana, +1 Charm Slot, multiple pick-one bonuses, 8 ascendancy points from trials.

**Note:** several rewards converted from passive points to **Weapon Set Points** in 0.2.0+; Maxroll's stats page still lists some as passive points — verify in-game `(unconfirmed which is live in 0.5.x)`.

|---|---|---|

| Charm choice | Ominous Altars (Freythorn) | Optional |

## Endgame-adjacent permanent bonuses

- **Martyr of the First Edict** — +1 passive point.
- **Expedition 10-slot remnants** — pick one each, eventually all: Olroth's Boon (+1 passive point), Vorana's Boon (+5% all Ele Res), Uhtred's Boon (+10 Spirit), Medved's Boon (+5% max Life and Mana).
- **Ascendancy points:** 8 total from Sekhemas/Chaos trials — see [ascendancy-unlock.md](ascendancy-unlock.md).
