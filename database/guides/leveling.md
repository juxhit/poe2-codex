# Leveling Guide (Campaign)

> Acts 1–4 + 3 Interludes in 0.5.x; Acts 5–6 (Oriath) at 1.0 — see [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md)

## Universal tips

- **Skills are never missed:** Uncut gems let you pick any skill — try several; respeccing is cheap early (gold).
- Socket **2–3 support gems** into your main skill as soon as Jeweller's Orbs allow.
- Keep elemental resists topped up — −10% per act ([mechanics/defences.md](../mechanics/defences.md)); Glacial/Desert/Storm runes are the cheap fix ([items/runes-soulcores.md](../items/runes-soulcores.md)).
- Life on every piece of gear; the campaign is a life-stacking exercise.
- Flasks recharge by fighting — don't hoard charges ([mechanics/flasks-charms.md](../mechanics/flasks-charms.md)).
- Act rewards are **permanent** (+20% Fire/Cold/Lightning across the campaign, +Ailment Threshold in Act 3, etc.) — don't skip optional quests granting them.

!!! tip "Don't hoard while leveling"
    Transmute/Aug every upgrade and disenchant magic/rare junk — orbs are abundant, levels are not. Flasks recharge by fighting, so spend charges freely.

## Act walkthrough (highlights)

| Act | Theme | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Riverlands (Clearfell, Grim Tangle) | Farrow → Runeforging unlock ([crafting/runeforging.md](../crafting/runeforging.md)); Finn → Currency Exchange |
| 2 | Vast Desert (Sekhemas) | **Trial of the Sekhemas** available; Risu exchange NPC |
| 3 | Vaal jungle (Chaos trial) | **Trial of Chaos**; Alva exchange; Altars of Corruption |
| 4 | Ngamakanui archipelago | 8 non-linear islands (0.3.0); Greater orbs start dropping |
| Interludes ×3 | Bridge to endgame | Replaced Cruel difficulty — one pass to maps |

## Trials & ascendancy timing

- First trial right after Act 2/3 (points 1–2); second (points 3–4) before endgame — full walkthrough: [ascendancy-unlock.md](ascendancy-unlock.md).
