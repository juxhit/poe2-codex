## Respec (The Hooded One)

- Unlocked via **"Sorrow Among Stones"** (Act 1) — revives The Hooded One in Clearfell.
- Cost: gold per passive point, scaling with **character level and tree position**; **Ascendancy points refund at 5× cost**.

!!! note "Respec costs scale hard"
    Community-measured: ~600 gold/point @ lvl 27; ~8,450 @ ~lvl 90; full respec ~187k @ lvl 94; big endgame resets 230k–800k `(unconfirmed, no official formula)`. Refund edge nodes first — cost reflects tree position.



## Gambling vendors (gold)

| Location | Gambler |
|---|---|
| Act 1 Clearfell | **Finn** |
| Act 2 Ardura Caravan | **Risu** |
| Act 3 Ziggurat Encampment | **Alva** |
| Endgame town | **Bestel** |

- Stock: **+1 item per unique monster killed**; prices scale with character level — except **rings always 50,000 gold, amulets always 63,000 gold**.
- Gambled item level caps ~83–86 at ~lvl 94. **Omen of Gambling**: 50% chance next gamble is free.
- Expedition NPCs use **artifact currencies, not gold**: Dannig (Sun), Gwennen (Exotic Coins — blind weapons), Tujen (Broken Circle — haggling), Rog (Black Scythe — deterministic crafting chains).

## Disenchanting vs Salvage

| Action | Where | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Disenchant** (sell window) | Any vendor | Transmutation Shards (magic), Regal Shards (rare), Chance Shards (some items) — **no Artificer Shards** |
| **Salvage** | Salvage Bench (Renly's quest, Act 1) | Socketed → **Artificer's Shards** (10 = 1 Artificer's Orb); quality → Whetstones/Scraps; superior+socketed gives both |
| **Uniques** | — | Generally cannot be salvaged `(unconfirmed)`; special salvage outcomes exist |

Example: 2-socket 5-quality blue chest → 2 Artificer + 1 Transmutation + 1 Armourer shard.

## Hideouts

- First hideout via **Alva** (Ziggurat Encampment, Act 3); **Shoreline Hideout** (Act 4) via "Hostile Takeover" — **Ange** moves in.
- Other hideouts: clear & claim areas ("clear these areas of their hostile inhabitants"; monsters at least Magic rarity). **41 hideout types** exist (Canopy, Canal, Black Cathedral, Sunken Palace, Kraken's Cove…).
- **Alva in hideout** = swap between unlocked hideouts; **Ange** = Currency Exchange + async trading (Merchant Tabs).
- Placeable/invited NPCs (Decorations → NPCs): Alva, Ange, Navali, Doryani, Zelina, Zolin, Hilda, Jado, Farrow, Recruiter Ann, Trialmaster + supporter merchants. Known bug: dismissed NPCs can't be re-placed.
