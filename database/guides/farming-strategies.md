## Goal-based presets

### Currency (raw orbs)
- Atlas: pack size + rarity nodes; Waystone mods: Rarity ≥10% + Pack Size; juice with Delirium mirrors at 200%.
- Mechanics: Ritual (omens) + Abyss (bones for Desecration crafting) — sell the results ([crafting/fractured-desecration.md](../crafting/fractured-desecration.md)).

### Uniques & bosses
- Farm **King in the Mists** (Rite of the Nameless) for Ritual uniques; **Sekhemas with unique relics** for guaranteed Zarokh drops ([mechanics/trials.md](../mechanics/trials.md)); **Citadels** for Uber act-boss uniques — see [bosses/pinnacle-bosses.md](../bosses/pinnacle-bosses.md).
- Simulacrum 7-wave → Delirium pinnacle key.

### Gold (respec/gambling/wages)
- Gold scales with quantity: **T16 magic-find waystones + shrines (Seeking: +100% rarity)** + "of the Devoted"-style mods; gold drops from all kills — see [vendors-hideout.md](vendors-hideout.md) for what it buys.

### XP to 100
- 200%+ Delirium maps (highest monster density × XP multipliers); enlightening shrines (+100% XP); avoid 10% death penalty by playing safe mods — death costs 10% of current-level XP from ~lvl 65 ([mechanics/death-trade-party.md](../mechanics/death-trade-party.md)).

## Tablet combos (Precursor Tablets, 3 slots on Map Device)

- **Same-type stacking amplifies** — e.g. two Breach tablets stack Breach spawn rates; add an Abyss tablet for cross-mechanic density.
- "Unforeseen Consequences" tablet + pack-size tablet = the standard juiced-run base; "Energized Ley Lines" doubles effects.
- Fracturing Orbs and some tablet crafts require Atlas passive specialization — [mechanics/atlas-waystones.md](../mechanics/atlas-waystones.md).

## Atlas presets

- Dedicated Atlas trees exist per league mechanic (Runes of Aldur tree added 0.5.4; Breach = Monastery of the Keepers; Ritual = Caer Tarth).
- Generic currency preset: waystone drop chance nodes → pack size → rarity → your 2 chosen mechanics' nodes → shrine nodes.
- Full per-mechanic node maps: `(open gap)` — pull from Maxroll Atlas sections when expanding.

## Waystone selection quick rules

- Identify **only for the drop-chance trick** when sustain is fine; otherwise identify and reroll for Quantity/Rarity/Pack Size.
- Regex filters for rerolls: [cheatsheets/regex.md](../cheatsheets/regex.md).
- 3 same-tier → 1 higher tier at the Reforging Bench; bosses drop +1 tier.
