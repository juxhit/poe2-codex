# Campaign Optimal Route (Acts 1–4 + Interludes)

> Source: [Maxroll Campaign Guide](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/path-of-exile-2-campaign-guide). Quest rewards: [campaign-quests.md](campaign-quests.md). Tips: [leveling.md](leveling.md)

## Act 1 — Ogham (town: Clearfell)

Vendors: Renly (weapons/armor), Una (caster + disenchant), Finn (gold/gambling), **Farrow → Runeforging** ("The Runeseeker" quest).

1. The Riverbank (Bloated Miller) → Clearfell (**Beira: +10% Cold Res**) → Mud Burrow (Devourer, Support Gem) → Grelwood → Red Vale (Rust King).
2. Grelwood (Tree of Souls) → Grim Tangle (Ervig) → Cemetery of the Eternals → Tomb of the Consort (Asinia) → Mausoleum (Draven) → Cemetery (Lachlann).
3. **Freythorn (King in the Mists: +30 Spirit)** → Hunting Grounds (**Crowbell: +2 points**) → Farmlands (**Una's Lute: +2 points**) → Ogham Village (**Renly's tools = Salvage Bench**) → Manor Ramparts → Ogham Manor (**Candlemass: +20 Life** → **Count Geonor** act boss).

## Act 2 — Vastiri (towns: Vastiri Outskirts → Ardura Caravan; **Risu** joins after Rudja)

1. Mawdun Quarry → Mawdun Mine (Rudja) → Traitor's Passage (**Balbala → Djinn Barya, unlocks Sekhemas**) → Halani Gates (Jamanra, the Risen King).
2. Mastodon Badlands → Bone Pits (**Sun Clan Relic**) → Keth (**Kabala: +2 points**) → Valley of the Titans (**Ancient Vows: +1 Charm Slot**).
3. Lost City → Buried Shrines (Azarian) → Titan Grotto (Zalmarath) → Deshar → Spires of Deshar (Tor Gul; **grab +10% Lightning Res shrine**) → Dreadnought (**Jamanra, the Abomination** act boss).

## Act 3 — Utzaal (towns: Sandswept Marsh → Ziggurat Encampment; **Alva**)

1. Sandswept Marsh (Rootdredge; Orok Basket = Jeweller's Orb) → Jungle Ruins (**Mighty Silverfist: +2 points**) → **Venom Crypts (Draught — PERMANENT pick)**.
2. Infested Barrens → Chimeral Wetlands (Xyclucian; side zone **Temple of Chaos = Trial of Chaos**) → Jiquani's Machinarium (**Blackjaw: +10% Fire Res**) → Jiquani's Sanctum (Zicoatl).
3. Matlan Waterways → **Azak Bog (Ignagduk: +30 Spirit)** → Drowned City → **Molten Vault (Mektul = Reforging Bench, 4-min timer)** → Apex of Filth → Temple of Kopec (Ketzuli).
4. Utzaal Past (Viper Napuatzi; Sacrificial Heart) → **Aggorat Past (Blood Sacrifice: +2 points)** → Black Chambers → **Doryani, Royal Thaumaturge** act finale.

## Act 4 — Kingsmarch (non-linear; town: Dannig/Rog/Tujen gambling, **Ange** exchange, Makoru sailing)

1. Isle of Kin (Blank Greater Rune) → Volcanic Warrens (Krutog) → Kedge Bay → Journey's End (**Omniphobia: +2 points**) → Abandoned Prison (**Goddess of Justice flask buff**) → Solitary Confinement.
2. Whakapanu Island (Great White One → **Kaimana Lesson, PERMANENT**) → Singing Caverns (Diamora) → Shrike Island → Eye of Hinekora (**Silent Hall: +5% Mana**).
3. Halls of the Dead (**3 Tattoos PERMANENT**; **Yama: +2 points**).
4. Arastas (Torvian) → The Excavation (Benedictus) → Ngakanu → Heart of the Tribe (**Tavakai** act boss).

## Interludes (removed when Acts 5–6 ship at 1.0)

**I1 Curse of Holten** (town: The Refuge): Scorched Farmlands → Stones of Serle → Blackwood → Holten (Ferryman sells Greater Runes) → Wolvenhold (**Oswin: +2 points**) → Holten Estate (pull Elswyth solo).

**I2 The Stolen Barya** (town: Khari Bazaar): Khari Crossing (**Akthi & Anundr: +2 points**; **Molten Shrine: +5% Life**) → Pools of Khatal → Sel Khari Sanctuary → Galai Gates → **Qimah (Seven Pillars buff)** → Qimah Reservoir (Azmadi).

**I3 Doryani's Contingency** (town: The Glade): Ashen Forest → Kriar Village (**Lythara: +40 Spirit**) → Glacial Tarn → Howling Caves (**Yeti: +2 points**) → Kriar Peaks (**Elder Madox: free unique**) → Etched Ravine → Cuachic Vault.

**Finish:** return Kingsmarch → Hooded One "Siege of Oriath" (**+2 points**) → Oriath → **endgame/Atlas**.

## Rules of thumb

- Do optional bosses with permanent bonuses before leaving their zone (list: [campaign-quests.md](campaign-quests.md)).
- Trials: Sekhemas unlock Act 2, Chaos Act 3 — complete both early for ascendancy points.
- Zones are semi-open; the above order minimizes backtracking for quest turn-ins.
