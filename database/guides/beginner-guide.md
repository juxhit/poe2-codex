# Beginner Guide

## Character creation

- Pick a class by feel, not fear — every class can use any skill gem; identity = attributes + starting tree position + ascendancies ([classes/](../classes/)).
- Hybrids (Monk, Mercenary, Druid) have flexible trees; pure classes (Warrior, Ranger, Witch, Sorceress) are more focused.
- Realm choice: Softcore vs Hardcore vs SSF ([mechanics/death-trade-party.md](../mechanics/death-trade-party.md)).

## First hours

1. **Skills come from Uncut Skill Gems** — you choose which skill to learn; nothing is missable.
2. Support gems socket **directly into skill gems** (no links) — socket count grows via Jeweller's Orbs.
3. Add support gems that match your damage type; don't spread across types early ([mechanics/damage-types.md](../mechanics/damage-types.md)).
4. **Resistances matter from Act 2** — the campaign applies −10% res per act ([mechanics/defences.md](../mechanics/defences.md)).
5. Dodge roll (Space) avoids strikes/projectiles but NOT AoE; hold it to sprint ([mechanics/movement-controls.md](../mechanics/movement-controls.md)).
6. Flasks recharge by fighting, not by kills ([mechanics/flasks-charms.md](../mechanics/flasks-charms.md)).
7. Buy waystones/learn vendors as they appear; Finn (Act 1) runs the Currency Exchange.

## Campaign (Acts 1–4 + Interludes)

- Follow the main quest; side areas give permanent stat bonuses (act rewards).
- Act bosses drop guaranteed uncut gems and upgrade currency.
- Act 3: **Trial of Chaos** appears; Act 2: **Trial of the Sekhemas** — either unlocks ascendancy ([guides/ascendancy-unlock.md](ascendancy-unlock.md)).
- Skip nothing mandatory: Interludes (0.3.0+) replaced Cruel difficulty — one campaign pass to endgame.

## First ascendancy

- Complete a trial right after Act 2/3 — 4 ascendancy points are the biggest power spike of the campaign.
- Read your class's ascendancy table before choosing: [classes/](../classes/) → ascendancy files.

## Endgame handoff

- Doryani's "Cataclysm's Wake" starts the endgame ([guides/endgame-progression.md](endgame-progression.md)).
- Death in maps costs 10% of current-level XP from ~level 65.
