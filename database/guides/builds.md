## Endgame meta (non-starter)

- Mobalytics S-tier endgame: Twister variants, Companion Beast Master, **Infernalist CoC Comet** / low-life crit multi / spell totem variants; Gemling "unexpectedly strong for deep endgame pushes".
- Currency strategies ([Maxroll endgame farming tier list](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/tierlists/endgame-farming-tier-list), 0.5.5): S-tier — **200% Delirium + Abyss, Tablet Farming, Ritual**; A-tier — Delirium Boss Rush, Breach rare-farming, Citadel Hunting.

## 1.0 league start (Dec 11, 2026)

- No official 1.0 tier list yet. Community consensus: wait for patch notes; Duelist/Shadow untested. Fallback picks: **Ice Shot Deadeye**, **Thunder Strike Oracle** `(unconfirmed)`, Twister/Companion builds.
