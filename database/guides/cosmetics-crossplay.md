# Cosmetics, Challenges & Cross-Play

> Sources: [official 0.5.0 notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540), [official Forbidden Rites event](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3999865), [Aoeah challenges list](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4798--poe-2-055-forbidden-rites-challenges-list-rewards--how-to-get), [PoE Vault crossplay](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/news/path-of-exile-2-crossplay-and-cross-progression-explained), [official sub-account thread](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3589725)

## League challenges

**PoE2 does NOT use PoE1's 40-challenge system.** Confirmed 0.5.x standard: milestone rewards at **2 / 4 / 6 / 8 challenges**.

- **Runes of Aldur league (0.5.0):** Knight of Aldur armour set pieces (helmet/gloves/boots/body) + one Runes of Aldur Totem hideout decoration per challenge.
- **Forbidden Rites event (0.5.5, ends with 1.0)** — 8 event challenges:
  1. The Riteseeker (11) — mandatory campaign Ritual encounters
  2. The Hunter (15) — 15 named campaign rare monsters
  3. The Ascendant (4) — all 8 ascendancy points
  4. The Master (1) — reach level 90
  5. The Reliquarian (50) — 50 uniques from Forbidden Rites Rituals
  6. The Nameless (18) — use 18 different Ritual Omens
  7. The Cartographer (3) — fully allocate 3 Atlas trees (Ritual/Delirium/Abyss)
  8. The Vanquisher (3) — defeat The Aberration, Xesht, Atziri the Red Queen
- Rewards: 2 → Locust Footprints; 4 → **Locust Swarm finisher effect**; 6 → Wildwood Guardian Pet; 8 → Wildwood Affliction extra effect; + totem piece per challenge. Mystery boxes advertised in the event but not tied to documented milestones `(unconfirmed)`.

## 1.0 monetization baseline

- F2P from Dec 11, 2026; EA supporter packs retired; purchases carry over permanently — see [1.0/confirmed.md](../1.0/confirmed.md). New core packs: Archon of Rebirth $60 → Corruption $480.

## Cross-play & cross-progression

- **Cross-play:** PC (Steam/Epic/standalone) + **PS5** + **Xbox Series X|S**.
- **Cross-progression:** link Steam/PSN/Xbox to one PoE account — **characters, stash, items, league progress all carry** between platforms.
- **The big caveat — MTX:**
  - **PSN purchases stay on Sony platforms**: on PC/Xbox they appear as **remove-only stash tabs** (withdraw only).
  - **PC ↔ Xbox:** MTX usable interchangeably with the correct **sub-account linked**; GGG Support can transfer between sub-accounts if cosmetics don't show.
  - **Supporter packs are platform-specific** — bought on PC ≠ cosmetics on PS5 (EA-era policy; expected to change with 1.0 F2P) `(unconfirmed post-1.0)`.
- League progress/challenge rewards are **account-side**, not platform-side.
- Guild/guild-stash cross-platform behavior not clearly documented `(unconfirmed)`; hideouts/decorations follow the account `(unconfirmed)`.
